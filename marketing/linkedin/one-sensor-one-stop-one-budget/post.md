"Keep trying until it works" is not a loop. It's a bill.
Give every agent loop one sensor: a test or a URL that measures the goal.
Add one stop condition and one budget: 3 iterations, then escalate to a human.
Observe → act → verify → update, and never keep trying without a meter.

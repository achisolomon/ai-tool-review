Rules you typed in chat don't survive compaction.
Compaction writes a summary, and a summary drops the "never edit db/migrations/" you said an hour ago.
CLAUDE.md is re-loaded on its own, so rules written there come back every time.
If a rule matters every session, it belongs in a file, not in chat.

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>AutoGPT pioneered the autonomous AI agent category with its ability to break down complex goals into subtasks and execute them independently. The platform now offers AutoPilot, which learns workflows through natural conversation, automatically builds specialized agents, and handles recurring tasks with minimal human intervention. It combines the flexibility of conversational briefing with the power of persistent automation.</p>
</div>

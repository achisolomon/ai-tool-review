## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Paperclip represents a paradigm shift in AI agent management, treating agent orchestration as an organizational challenge rather than just a technical one. It enables businesses to manage entire teams of AI agents with familiar enterprise concepts like org charts, budgets, governance policies, and strategic goals—all within a single deployment. Unlike traditional agent frameworks that focus on individual agent capabilities, Paperclip provides the infrastructure to coordinate multiple agents across business functions (development, content, social media, operations) with proper oversight, resource allocation, and accountability. The platform addresses the emerging need for AI agents that operate not in isolation but as coordinated teams serving business objectives.</p>
</div>

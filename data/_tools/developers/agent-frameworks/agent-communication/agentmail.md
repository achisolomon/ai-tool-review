## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>AgentMail is an API-first email platform designed specifically for building AI agents that can communicate over email. Unlike traditional email APIs built for one-way notifications, AgentMail is built for two-way conversations — agents can read, understand, and reply to emails in a thread just like a human. Spin up a new inbox in milliseconds with no domain verification required. The platform raised $6M seed funding led by General Catalyst, with participation from Y Combinator and angel investors including Paul Graham, Dharmesh Shah (HubSpot CTO), Paul Copplestone (Supabase CEO), and Karim Atiyeh (Ramp CTO).</p>
</div>

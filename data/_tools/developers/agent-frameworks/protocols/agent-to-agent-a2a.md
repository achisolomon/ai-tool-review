## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Agent-to-Agent (A2A) is Google's open-source protocol designed to standardize communication between autonomous AI agents. It enables agents from different systems to discover each other, negotiate capabilities, and collaborate on complex tasks across organizational boundaries. The protocol focuses on security, trust verification, and seamless interoperability, making it possible for agents to work together regardless of their underlying implementation or hosting environment.</p>
</div>

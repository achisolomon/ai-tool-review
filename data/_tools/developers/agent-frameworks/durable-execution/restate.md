## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Restate is a lightweight runtime that transforms AI agents, workflows, and backend services into durable processes with automatic reliability. The platform's core philosophy is enabling developers to focus on business logic while Restate handles all the complex failure mechanics behind the scenes. Built with a cloud-native architecture, it provides durable execution capabilities without the operational overhead of traditional workflow engines. Early users report being "absolutely shocked" at the ease of setup, with financial transaction workflows built and running with confidence in under a week. Restate enables serverless durable execution, making it particularly powerful for teams that want reliability without managing complex infrastructure.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Jellyfish is a Software Engineering Intelligence Platform designed for R&D organizations looking to measure and optimize the real impact of their engineering investments—including AI coding tools like GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and Claude. It aggregates data from 50+ developer tools (Jira, GitHub, GitLab, CI/CD pipelines, AI assistants) to provide actionable insights on team health, delivery velocity, cycle time, and resource allocation. The platform specifically addresses AI tool ROI tracking, helping engineering leaders understand whether their AI investments are actually driving higher productivity.</p>
</div>

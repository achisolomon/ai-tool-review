## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Port is an Agentic SDLC Platform that functions as an Internal Developer Portal (IDP), giving engineering teams a unified system for managing software catalogs, workflows, and AI-powered automation. The platform provides a Context Lake for centralized data, Scorecards for measuring engineering standards and quality metrics, and autonomous AI agents that can handle ticket resolution, incident remediation, and resource provisioning. For teams measuring AI tool impact, Port's scorecards can track compliance with engineering standards and connect to velocity metrics through its integrations.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Base44 is an all-in-one, AI-powered no-code app builder that turns plain-English descriptions into fully functional full-stack applications. Instead of wiring together separate services, Base44 bundles the entire stack — database, user authentication, email, analytics, file storage, hosting, and deployment — so builders never touch external integrations. Founded in early 2025 by solo bootstrapper Maor Shlomo, it hit $1M ARR three weeks after launch and was acquired by Wix for roughly $80M just six months later. It's aimed at non-technical founders, product people, and developers who want to go from idea to deployed app in minutes.</p>
</div>

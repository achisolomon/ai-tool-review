## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Google Antigravity is Google's agent-first integrated development environment, released November 2025 alongside Gemini 3. Unlike traditional AI coding assistants that help you write code, Antigravity deploys autonomous AI agents that can plan, execute, and verify complex engineering tasks with minimal human intervention. The platform features a Manager Surface for orchestrating multiple agents working asynchronously, a Browser Subagent that actually uses your app as it builds it, and parallel sub-agents for tackling complex tasks simultaneously.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Factory is an agent-native software development platform that deploys autonomous AI agents called "Droids" to handle the full development lifecycle. Unlike autocomplete tools, Droids are specialized agents that work in parallel: a Writer Droid generates code, a Reviewer Droid evaluates PRs, a Tester Droid handles tests, a Docs Droid creates documentation, and a Deployer Droid manages releases. The platform is model-agnostic and interface-agnostic, working across terminal, IDE, Slack, Linear, and browsers. Backed by Khosla Ventures, Sequoia, NVIDIA, and others at a $1.5B valuation, Factory is used by engineering teams at NVIDIA, Adobe, MongoDB, and Zapier.</p>
</div>

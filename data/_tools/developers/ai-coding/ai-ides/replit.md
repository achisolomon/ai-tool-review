## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Replit is a browser-based IDE that lets you write, run, and deploy code instantly from any device. It combines collaborative coding, instant hosting, and AI assistance through Ghostwriter (code completion and chat) and Replit Agent (autonomous app builder). Originally popular for education and learning, Replit has evolved into a full development platform where you can build, share, and deploy web apps without any local setup. The zero-configuration environment supports 50+ languages including Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Go, Rust, and more.</p>
</div>

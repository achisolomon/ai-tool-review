## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Windsurf is Codeium's AI-native IDE built on a VS Code fork with deep AI integration at its core. Unlike traditional IDEs with AI bolted on, Windsurf was designed from the ground up around its Cascade agentic system, which can understand context across your entire codebase, suggest multi-file edits, and execute terminal commands autonomously. The IDE offers a unique "Flows" paradigm that combines the best of AI-assisted coding with human oversight, maintaining awareness of what you're doing while proactively suggesting next steps.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>OpenSpec introduces an artifact-guided workflow for spec-driven development, emphasizing alignment between humans and AI before code implementation. Unlike rigid phase-gate methodologies, OpenSpec allows fluid updates to any artifact (proposals, specs, designs, tasks) at any time, maintaining organization through dedicated change folders. Each feature or modification gets its own structured workspace, enabling teams to stay organized while preserving flexibility in how they approach specification and implementation.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Coder is a self-hosted platform that provisions cloud development environments on your own infrastructure. It enables developers and AI coding agents to work together in secure, standardized workspaces with full governance and policy controls. Built on Terraform, Coder allows you to define repeatable development environments as code, deploy them in the cloud or on-premises, and give your team instant access to properly configured workspaces from any IDE.</p>
</div>

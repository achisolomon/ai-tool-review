## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Overcut brings autonomous AI agents into your engineering workflows, coordinating seamlessly across tools like Jira, Linear, ClickUp, GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, and Azure DevOps. The platform automates repetitive engineering tasks such as PR reviews, ticket triage, and specification generation, allowing teams to scale their development processes without proportionally scaling headcount. By creating intelligent workflows that span multiple tools, repositories, and processes, Overcut helps engineering teams maintain velocity while reducing manual overhead.</p>
</div>

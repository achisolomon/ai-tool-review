## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Lumia Security is an AI Usage Control platform operating at the network layer that gives enterprises visibility and governance over how employees and autonomous agents interact with AI systems. Rather than a pure LLM proxy focused on token cost allocation, Lumia positions itself as a security and compliance layer — intercepting AI traffic to detect sensitive data exposure, enforce usage policies, and audit agentic workflows. Its proprietary Protocol Analysis Engine performs content-level risk assessment on AI interactions. The platform targets CISOs and IT security leadership at enterprises navigating AI adoption at scale. Pricing is not publicly disclosed; contact via sales demo.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Protect AI is an end-to-end MLSecOps platform that secures machine learning systems across the entire AI lifecycle. The platform includes Guardian for model vulnerability scanning, Recon for AI asset discovery, Layer for runtime protection, and Radar for continuous threat monitoring. Protect AI also operates Huntr, the world's first bug bounty platform for AI/ML vulnerabilities, and maintains the open-source LLM Guard project. The company drives the MLSecOps community forward through educational resources, tooling, and expert insights for AI security leaders.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Robust Intelligence, acquired by Cisco in October 2024, pioneered the AI security category with cutting-edge research including algorithmic red teaming and the industry's first AI Firewall. Now foundational to Cisco AI Defense and Foundation AI, the platform provides network-layer visibility across enterprise AI traffic, enforcing security without agents or libraries. It protects against prompt injection, model DoS, code detection, and off-topic attacks with live threat intelligence from Cisco's AI research lab and Talos. The solution decouples AI development from security, enabling teams to build while security controls are managed at the network level.</p>
</div>

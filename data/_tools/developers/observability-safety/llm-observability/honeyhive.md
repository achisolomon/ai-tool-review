## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>HoneyHive helps teams test and monitor AI agents across the development lifecycle. It traces agent runs through OpenTelemetry-based SDKs, runs automated evaluators and human review on those traces, and turns failures into datasets for regression testing in CI. Prompt versioning and deployment let teams manage prompts outside the codebase and roll changes out safely.</p>
<p>Founded in New York in 2022 by Mohak Sharma and Dhruv Singh, HoneyHive raised $7.4M across a pre-seed and a $5.5M seed led by Insight Partners. It emphasizes enterprise deployment options: the paid plan offers self-hosting, hybrid or single-tenant setups, custom data retention, and a DPA and BAA for regulated industries.</p>
</div>

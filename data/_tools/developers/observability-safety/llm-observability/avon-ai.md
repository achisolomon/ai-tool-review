## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Avon AI sits on top of AI agents that are already talking to customers. It reads their conversations and flags policy violations, hallucinations and behavioral drift, gives business owners (not just engineers) a way to correct the agent's knowledge and behavior in plain language, and keeps testing the agent against the company's own rules so regressions are caught early. It works with agents from any vendor, so a company running several agent platforms can manage them in one place.</p>
<p>The Tel Aviv company targets enterprises in regulated industries such as banking, fintech and insurance. Customers named on its site include eToro, OneZero Bank, ChargeAfter and ApartmentList. It holds SOC 2 and ISO 27001, supports on-premises, private cloud and hybrid deployment, and was named in Gartner's Market Guide for Guardian Agents in February 2026.</p>
</div>

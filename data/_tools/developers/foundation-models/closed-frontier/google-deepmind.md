## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Google DeepMind is a leading AI research laboratory developing frontier foundation models including Gemini, the multimodal successor to PaLM and LaMDA. Known for breakthroughs like AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and advanced reasoning models, DeepMind's work spans from fundamental AI research to production systems powering Google's AI services with models featuring up to 2M token context windows.</p>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Reka AI develops natively multimodal foundation models that process video, images, audio, and text from the ground up. The platform offers a tiered model family (Spark, Edge, Flash, Core) optimized for different use cases, with particularly strong video understanding capabilities used by enterprises in security, media, and defense sectors.</p>

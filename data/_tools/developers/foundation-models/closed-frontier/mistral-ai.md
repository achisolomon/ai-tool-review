## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Mistral AI is a Paris-based artificial intelligence company founded in 2023 by former Meta and Google DeepMind researchers, including Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample, and Timothee Lacroix. The company has rapidly become Europe's leading AI lab, building both open-weight and commercial frontier models. Mistral's approach emphasizes efficiency, offering models that deliver GPT-4 class performance at significantly lower costs. Their product lineup spans from the flagship Mistral Large for complex reasoning to Codestral for code generation and Pixtral for vision tasks.</p>
</div>

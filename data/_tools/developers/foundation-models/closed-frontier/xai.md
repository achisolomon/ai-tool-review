## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>xAI is Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company focused on building AI systems that understand the universe. Their flagship Grok models combine frontier reasoning capabilities with real-time access to X (Twitter) data, offering unique insights into current events and trending topics. Grok-3, released in February 2025, represents a significant leap in performance, achieving state-of-the-art results on reasoning benchmarks like AIME and GPQA Diamond. The models are available through both the X platform (via Grok chat) and a developer API at competitive prices.</p>
</div>

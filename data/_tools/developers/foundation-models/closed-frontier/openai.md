## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>OpenAI is the company that started the generative AI revolution with ChatGPT in 2022 and remains the dominant force in foundation models. Their API platform provides access to the GPT-5.5 series (flagship multimodal models), o-series reasoning models (o3, o4-mini), and specialized tools for embeddings, image generation (DALL-E 3), video (Sora), and speech. With the largest ecosystem of integrations, fine-tuning capabilities, and enterprise adoption, OpenAI's platform is the default choice for developers building AI-powered applications. While competitors like Anthropic and Google have caught up on benchmarks, OpenAI's ecosystem breadth, brand recognition, and first-mover advantage keep it as the industry standard.</p>
</div>

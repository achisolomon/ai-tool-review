## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Cohere is an enterprise-focused AI company founded by former Google Brain researchers, including Aidan Gomez, co-author of the "Attention Is All You Need" paper that introduced the Transformer architecture. The platform specializes in production-ready language models optimized for business applications, particularly retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), semantic search, and text embeddings. Unlike consumer-focused AI products, Cohere targets enterprises needing secure, scalable, and compliant AI deployments with flexible hosting options including cloud, on-premise, and VPC deployments.</p>
</div>

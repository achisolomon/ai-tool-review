## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>01.AI (Zero One Everything) develops the Yi series of open-source foundation models ranging from 6B to 34B parameters with exceptional multilingual capabilities. Founded by Kai-Fu Lee, the models feature 200K context windows, strong performance on reasoning tasks, and support for both Chinese and English, offering Apache 2.0 licensing for commercial use.</p>

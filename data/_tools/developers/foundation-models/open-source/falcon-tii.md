## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Falcon is the Technology Innovation Institute's (UAE) family of open-source large language models featuring the Falcon 3 series with multimodal capabilities. The models are optimized for efficiency, capable of running on laptops without sacrificing performance, and support multiple languages with resource-efficient deployment, including the innovative Falcon Mamba state-space model architecture.</p>

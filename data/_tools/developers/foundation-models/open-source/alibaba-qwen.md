## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen) is Alibaba Cloud's series of open-source foundation models spanning 0.5B to 72B parameters with strong multilingual and multimodal capabilities. The family includes text-only, vision-language, and audio models, with particularly strong performance in Chinese, mathematics, and coding tasks, available under Apache 2.0 license.</p>

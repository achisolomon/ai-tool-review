## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Portkey is a comprehensive AI gateway platform that helps enterprises build reliable, production-grade AI applications. Supporting 250+ LLM providers with a unified API, Portkey offers advanced features including intelligent routing, fallback handling, guardrails for safety and compliance, detailed observability, and collaborative prompt management. Trusted by companies worldwide, Portkey simplifies LLM operations with enterprise-grade reliability, security, and cost optimization tools in a single platform.</p>
</div>

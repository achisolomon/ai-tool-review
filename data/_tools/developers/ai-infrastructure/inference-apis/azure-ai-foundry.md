## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Azure AI Foundry is Microsoft's enterprise platform for building, deploying, and managing AI agents and applications. It integrates with Azure OpenAI, Copilot Studio, and other Azure services, providing end-to-end infrastructure for AI development with built-in safety, compliance, and monitoring capabilities.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Cerebras delivers the world's fastest AI inference powered by custom wafer-scale chips. Built on proprietary CS-3 systems with 900,000 cores each, Cerebras achieves record-breaking throughput up to 1,860 tokens/second for Llama 3.1-70B, making it 20x faster than traditional GPU solutions while maintaining competitive pricing starting at $0.60/M tokens.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>DeepInfra is a serverless inference platform offering access to 150+ open-source AI models including LLMs, vision models, embeddings, and speech-to-text. It provides cost-effective pay-per-token pricing starting at $0.06/M tokens alongside dedicated GPU deployments with A100 and H100 options. The platform emphasizes affordability and simplicity with no infrastructure management required.</p>
</div>

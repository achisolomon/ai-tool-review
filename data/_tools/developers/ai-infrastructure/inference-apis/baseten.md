## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Baseten is a specialized inference platform for deploying large language models with exceptional performance and cost efficiency. It optimizes for latency, throughput, and cost through intelligent batching, caching, and hardware allocation across global infrastructure. Designed for teams prioritizing inference reliability and efficiency.</p>

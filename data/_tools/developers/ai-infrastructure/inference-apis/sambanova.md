## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>SambaNova provides a complete AI platform built on proprietary Reconfigurable Dataflow Architecture (RDA) chips, delivering record-breaking inference speeds while being easy to integrate into existing data centers. The platform offers both cloud-hosted inference APIs and on-premise deployments, with particular strengths in agentic AI, fine-tuning, and enterprise-scale workloads requiring security and compliance.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>CoreWeave is a specialized cloud provider focused on GPU-accelerated infrastructure for AI and machine learning workloads. Founded in 2017, CoreWeave offers access to the latest NVIDIA GPUs including H100, A100, and A40 with Kubernetes-native orchestration. The platform provides bare metal performance with cloud flexibility, targeting AI researchers, ML engineers, and enterprises building compute-intensive applications. CoreWeave has raised over $2B in funding and serves major AI companies.</p>
</div>

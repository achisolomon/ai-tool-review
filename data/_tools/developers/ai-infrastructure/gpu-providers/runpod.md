## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>RunPod is a GPU cloud platform designed for AI developers, offering both on-demand pods and serverless GPU compute. With deployment times under 30 seconds, RunPod provides access to 30+ GPU models including H100, A100, and RTX series across 31 global regions. The platform features auto-scaling serverless endpoints, flexible spot and on-demand pricing, and zero idle costs for serverless deployments. RunPod's simple container-based workflow makes it popular among ML engineers and AI startups.</p>
</div>

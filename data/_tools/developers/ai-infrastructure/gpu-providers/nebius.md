## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Nebius is a European AI cloud infrastructure platform that emerged from Yandex Cloud in 2023. The company provides GPU-accelerated computing with NVIDIA H100, H200, and A100 clusters optimized for AI training and inference. With data centers in Europe and expanding globally, Nebius offers competitive pricing, flexible scaling from single GPUs to thousand-GPU clusters, and EU data residency options. The platform supports major ML frameworks and provides both on-demand and reserved capacity.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Lambda Labs is a GPU cloud and hardware company built specifically for deep learning and AI workloads. Unlike general-purpose cloud providers, Lambda focuses exclusively on machine learning infrastructure, offering on-demand cloud GPUs, pre-configured workstations, and servers optimized for training and inference. Their Lambda Stack provides a one-line installation of popular ML frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, CUDA) that works seamlessly across their cloud and on-premise hardware. Known for competitive pricing and simplicity, Lambda is popular among AI researchers, startups, and enterprises who need GPU compute without the complexity of hyperscaler clouds.</p>
</div>

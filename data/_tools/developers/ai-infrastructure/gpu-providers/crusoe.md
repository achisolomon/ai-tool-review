## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Crusoe is a sustainable AI cloud infrastructure provider that powers data centers using stranded or wasted energy sources like flared natural gas. Founded in 2018, Crusoe offers competitive GPU computing with NVIDIA H100 and A100 GPUs while reducing carbon emissions by up to 80%. The company focuses on energy efficiency and sustainability without compromising performance, making it ideal for organizations prioritizing environmental impact alongside computational power.</p>
</div>

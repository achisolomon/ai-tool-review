## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Vast.ai operates a peer-to-peer GPU rental marketplace connecting users with community-hosted machines worldwide. Unlike traditional cloud providers, Vast.ai aggregates spare GPU capacity from data centers and individual hosts, offering prices starting as low as $0.10/hr. The platform supports a wide range of GPUs from RTX 3060 to H100, with instant availability and flexible rental periods. Popular among researchers, students, and small teams prioritizing cost over enterprise features.</p>
</div>

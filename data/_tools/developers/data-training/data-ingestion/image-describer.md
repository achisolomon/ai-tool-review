## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Image Describer analyzes uploaded images and generates text descriptions for accessibility, SEO, content, and other uses. It supports multiple output formats, including short and detailed descriptions, WCAG-compliant alt text, image prompts for Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and Flux, social captions and hashtags, multi-language output, and OCR text extraction. Batch processing lets users analyze multiple images at once rather than one at a time.</p>
</div>

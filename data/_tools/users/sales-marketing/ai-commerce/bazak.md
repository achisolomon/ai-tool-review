## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Bazak is an AI commerce platform, built by Tel Aviv-based Bazak AI Innovation Ltd., that turns a brand's product catalog into a single conversational agent deployed everywhere a customer might shop: ChatGPT via plugins, Claude via connectors, MCP-based tool ecosystems, and the brand's own website. The same agent carries context across all three stages of the relationship: it sells using live stock and pricing data inside the AI ecosystem, hands off the conversation to the brand's own checkout to convert the sale, and then keeps serving the customer afterward, handling order status, returns, and support through that same conversation history. Bazak targets commerce-heavy verticals, including travel and hospitality, retail and e-commerce, financial services, insurance, and airlines, and reports customers such as Fattal, Israir, ZAP, and WallaShops.</p>
</div>

## Pros & Cons

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>One agent and context carried across ChatGPT, Claude, and the brand's own site, rather than separate bots per channel</li>
      <li>Native support for ChatGPT plugins, Claude connectors, and MCP, plus emerging agentic-commerce protocols (AP2, ACP, UCP, x402)</li>
      <li>Covers the full loop: AI-ecosystem discovery, on-site conversion, and post-purchase service with the same agent</li>
      <li>Reports a fast rollout, live production within about 4 business days of kickoff</li>
      <li>Reports measurable commerce outcomes: a 42% conversion lift on AI-referred traffic and a 12.5% on-site revenue lift</li>
    </ul>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>11x sells "digital workers" for sales teams. Alice is an AI sales development rep that researches prospects, writes personalized multichannel outreach (email and LinkedIn), handles replies and books meetings. Julian is an AI phone agent that qualifies inbound leads and makes outbound calls. Both connect to CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot.</p>
<p>Buyers should know the company's recent history. In March 2025 TechCrunch published an investigation, based on about two dozen sources, reporting that 11x had listed companies as customers that said they weren't (ZoomInfo threatened legal action over its logo) and that its revenue figures counted contracts customers had already exited. In May 2025 founder Hasan Sukkar moved to non-executive chairman and CTO Prabhav Jain became CEO. The product is still sold and developed, but it's worth verifying references carefully.</p>
</div>

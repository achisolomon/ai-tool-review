## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Eightfold AI sells a talent intelligence platform to large employers. Its models are trained on a very large set of career profiles and skills, which it uses to match candidates to open roles, rediscover past applicants, recommend internal moves and plan workforce skills. Newer products add AI agents: an AI Interviewer that screens candidates, an Interview Companion that supports recruiters during interviews, and a Candidate Agent that guides applicants, aimed especially at high-volume hourly hiring.</p>
<p>Founded in 2016 by Ashu Garg and Varun Kacholia, Eightfold raised a $220M Series E in 2021 led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 at a $2.1B valuation. Customers include large enterprises such as Salesforce, Citi and HP, and the platform holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 42001 and FedRAMP Moderate authorization, which matters for government and regulated buyers.</p>
</div>

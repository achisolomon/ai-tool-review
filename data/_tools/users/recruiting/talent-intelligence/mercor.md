## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Mercor started as an AI recruiting marketplace, using AI interviews to vet and match remote software engineers with companies. It has since become best known as an expert network for AI labs: it recruits domain professionals such as physicians, lawyers, bankers, consultants and engineers, screens them with AI interviews, and pays them hourly to create training data and evaluate model outputs in their field. It also publishes APEX, a benchmark that tests frontier models on real professional tasks.</p>
<p>Founded in 2023 by Brendan Foody, Adarsh Hiremath and Surya Midha, Mercor raised a $350M Series C in October 2025 at a $10B valuation. TechCrunch reported it was paying out more than $1.5M a day to experts, with frontier labs among its main customers.</p>
</div>

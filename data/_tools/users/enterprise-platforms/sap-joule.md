## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>SAP Joule is SAP's generative AI copilot, designed to transform how users interact with SAP's enterprise software portfolio. Launched in 2023 and continuously expanded through 2024-2026, Joule is embedded natively across SAP's cloud applications including S/4HANA Cloud, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur, and SAP Business Technology Platform. Unlike standalone AI assistants, Joule operates within SAP's trusted business context, understanding your company's specific data, processes, and workflows. Users can ask questions in natural language, get intelligent insights, automate repetitive tasks, and navigate complex business processes without deep SAP expertise. Joule leverages SAP's decades of business process knowledge combined with modern large language models to deliver contextual, role-based AI assistance across finance, HR, procurement, supply chain, and customer experience.</p>
</div>

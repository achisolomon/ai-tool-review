## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Cohere North is an enterprise AI workspace built on Cohere's foundation models, designed to transform scattered company knowledge into an intelligent assistant employees can actually trust. Unlike consumer AI chatbots, North connects directly to your organization's data sources—Google Drive, Confluence, Slack, SharePoint, Notion, and 40+ integrations—to provide grounded answers with inline citations. Every response traces back to source documents, dramatically reducing hallucination risk. Built by the team behind the Transformer paper, North leverages Cohere's RAG-optimized Command R+ models and industry-leading Embed and Rerank technology to deliver accurate, contextual answers in 100+ languages. The platform offers flexible deployment options including cloud, VPC, and on-premise for organizations with strict data residency requirements.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Angelina AI builds "governed Agentic AI employees that work inside your systems": role-based AI agents it calls VEmployees, each designed for a specific job and connected to the tools an organization already uses. Angelina Studio is where teams design these digital employees around real workflows; Angelina OS supplies the identity, permissions, approvals, audit trails and governance needed to run them in production. The Tel Aviv company targets enterprises and the public sector, with vertical offerings for education, employment tech, healthcare administration and manufacturing, and site-listed customers including Beit Berl College and Digital Israel.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use Angelina AI?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Enterprises and public-sector bodies that need AI agents with audit trails and approvals</li>
        <li>Colleges and training organizations running Moodle or Canvas</li>
        <li>Regulated teams that need private-cloud or on-prem deployment</li>
        <li>Organizations wanting a guided 30-day pilot rather than a self-serve build</li>

    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>

        <li>Buyers who need public pricing or published compliance certifications up front</li>
        <li>Clinical decision-making (healthcare use is limited to administrative tasks)</li>
        <li>Teams that want a mature product with independent reviews and a long track record</li>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Governance built in: per-VEmployee RBAC and least-privilege identities, SSO (SAML/OIDC), PII redaction, tenant segregation</li>
      <li>Human-in-the-loop approvals, escalation policies and a kill switch</li>
      <li>Full trace from input to actions to outcomes, immutable logs, dashboards, alerts and SIEM export</li>
      <li>Cloud, private cloud or on-prem deployment, with optional data residency</li>
      <li>Connects to Moodle, Canvas, Monday.com, Jira, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, email, CRM and ERP via API and webhooks</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://www.angelina-ai.com/security" target="_blank">Source</a></div>

  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>No public pricing; you book a demo or a pilot</li>
      <li>No compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) named on the security page</li>
      <li>Underlying LLMs are not disclosed</li>
      <li>Young company (founded 2025) with no independent reviews or press coverage found</li>

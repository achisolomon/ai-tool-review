## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Workday Illuminate is Workday's AI platform that infuses artificial intelligence across its Human Capital Management (HCM) and Financial Management applications. Unlike standalone AI tools, Illuminate is deeply embedded into the Workday ecosystem, leveraging over 800 billion transactions annually to power intelligent recommendations, natural language search, and autonomous AI agents. The platform enables HR leaders to surface insights from workforce data, finance teams to automate journal entries and anomaly detection, and employees to interact with Workday using conversational queries. Announced in 2024, Illuminate represents Workday's push to transform enterprise resource management from transactional systems into intelligent, proactive platforms.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Salesforce Agentforce is a platform for building and deploying AI agents that work autonomously within the Salesforce ecosystem. Unlike standalone AI assistants, Agentforce agents operate with full context from your CRM data—customer history, account details, case records, and business processes—enabling them to take real actions, not just provide suggestions. Launched in late 2024 and rapidly expanded through 2025-2026, it represents Salesforce's push to move beyond copilots to fully autonomous AI workers that can handle sales outreach, customer service, marketing campaigns, and commerce interactions without human intervention.</p>
</div>

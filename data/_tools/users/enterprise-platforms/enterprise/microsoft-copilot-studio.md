## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Microsoft Copilot Studio is a low-code platform for building, customizing, and managing AI-powered copilots and chatbots that integrate deeply with the Microsoft ecosystem. Evolved from Power Virtual Agents, it enables organizations to create custom conversational AI agents without extensive coding, extend Microsoft 365 Copilot with company-specific knowledge, and deploy autonomous agents that can take actions across business systems. The platform leverages generative AI (GPT-4) for natural language understanding while providing enterprise-grade security through Microsoft's trust infrastructure, making it the go-to choice for organizations already invested in Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, and Power Platform.</p>
</div>

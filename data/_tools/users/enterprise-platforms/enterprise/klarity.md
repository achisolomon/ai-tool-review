## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Within is the new name of Klarity, a San Francisco company that started in document automation for finance and accounting teams (reading contracts and invoices for revenue recognition and order processing). It has since repositioned around enterprise AI transformation. Within observes how work actually happens across a company's applications, structures it into what it calls a Company Brain (a map of processes, handoffs and approvals), and uses that map to decide which tasks should stay with people and which can move to AI agents, then gives those agents company-specific context.</p>
<p>Klarity raised a $70M Series B in 2024. Accenture has invested through Accenture Ventures and partners with Within on client transformation work. The company cites analyzing thousands of processes at DoorDash in a matter of weeks.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>IBM watsonx Orchestrate is an enterprise AI platform that combines conversational AI with automation to help knowledge workers complete complex tasks through natural language. Part of IBM's watsonx AI and data platform launched in 2023, Orchestrate enables organizations to build AI assistants that can execute multi-step workflows across business applications like Salesforce, SAP, Workday, and Microsoft 365. Unlike simple chatbots, Orchestrate uses a skills-based architecture where pre-built and custom skills chain together to automate entire business processes—from HR onboarding to sales pipeline management. The platform supports multi-agent orchestration, allowing specialized AI agents to collaborate on complex enterprise tasks while maintaining security and compliance controls that regulated industries require.</p>
</div>

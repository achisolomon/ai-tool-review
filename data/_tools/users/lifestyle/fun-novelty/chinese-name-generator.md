## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Chinese Name Generator is an AI tool that composes a Chinese name from a few details about you. You enter your English name and can add a surname, age, occupation, gender, Simplified or Traditional script, a style (classic, modern, poetic or literary) and up to three personality traits. A language model proposes names and returns the Chinese characters, pinyin and English meaning, with a character-by-character breakdown and tone-harmony checks. There are separate pages for female and male names. It suits learners, expats, travelers and anyone who wants a Chinese name with real meaning instead of a phonetic transliteration.</p>
</div>

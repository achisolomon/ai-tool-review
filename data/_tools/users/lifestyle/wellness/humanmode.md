## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Get HumanMode is a digital wellness program for people who feel they lean on AI and screens too much. It starts with a free Focus Check (seven questions, about three minutes) that gives a focus score and a recommended plan, then runs a 14-Day Human Reset of daily lessons, reflection prompts, timers and habit trackers. Milestones include a 45-minute deep work session without AI on day 3, with the stated goal by day 14 of using AI as a deliberate tool rather than a reflex. It is aimed at knowledge workers, students, founders and creators, and it is built by a solo founder. The site describes itself as a wellness program, not therapy or medical care.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>TeachAny is an AI platform built to automate routine teaching tasks for educators, using current large language models to generate learning materials and personalized instruction support. Core features include quiz and worksheet generation, flashcard creation, lesson planning, math practice and spiral review, rubric generation, exercise and practice test creation, and text leveling, proofreading, and rewriting. It also covers more specialized needs like coloring page and maze generation, report card comments, recommendation letter writing, and differentiated instruction support for mixed-ability classrooms. The company is registered as DEEP EXPLORE PTE. LTD., and data is stored on US servers.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>MagicSchool is an AI platform built specifically for K-12 teachers. It offers more than 80 purpose-built tools for everyday teaching work: lesson plans, unit plans, rubrics, quizzes, differentiated reading passages, IEP drafts, parent emails, writing feedback and slide decks. Raina, its AI assistant, answers teaching questions. Student Rooms let teachers give students access to AI tools with guardrails, content moderation and full visibility into what students do.</p>
<p>Founded in 2023 by former teacher and principal Adeel Khan, MagicSchool grew to more than 6 million educators across 150+ countries. It raised a $45M Series B in January 2025 led by Valor Equity Partners. Teachers can use it free, upgrade to Plus, or get it through a district license with SSO and admin controls.</p>
</div>

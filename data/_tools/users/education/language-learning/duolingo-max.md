## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Duolingo Max is the highest subscription tier of Duolingo, the world's most popular language learning app. It includes everything in Super Duolingo (no ads, unlimited hearts, personalized practice) plus AI features built on OpenAI models. The headline feature is Video Call, a real-time spoken conversation with the character Lily that adapts to your level. Roleplay puts you in everyday scenarios, like ordering at a café or planning a trip, and gives feedback on your answers.</p>
<p>Max launched in 2023. Explain My Answer, originally a Max feature, became free for all users in January 2026, so Max's value now rests mainly on conversation practice. Max is offered for popular courses for English speakers, including Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean.</p>
</div>

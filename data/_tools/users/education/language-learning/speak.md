## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Speak is a language learning app designed around one idea: you learn to speak by speaking. Most of the lesson time is spent talking out loud. Its AI tutor holds open-ended conversations, role-plays real situations, corrects grammar and word choice, and gives instant feedback on pronunciation. Lessons are structured into a curriculum, with personalized review based on what you get wrong.</p>
<p>Speak started in South Korea teaching English and became one of the country's top education apps before expanding to other markets and languages, including Spanish for English speakers. The OpenAI Startup Fund is an investor and Speak has been an early partner for OpenAI's speech technology. In December 2024 it raised $78M led by Accel at a $1B valuation.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Khanmigo is the AI tutor and teaching assistant from Khan Academy, the nonprofit online learning organization. For students, it works like a patient tutor: instead of handing over answers, it asks guiding questions, gives hints and checks understanding as they work through math, science, reading, writing, computer science and test prep, all tied to Khan Academy's full K-12 content library. It can also play roles such as a historical figure or a debate partner.</p>
<p>For teachers, Khanmigo is free in supported countries and acts as an assistant: it drafts lesson plans, rubrics, exit tickets and quiz questions, differentiates materials, and gives insight into student progress. Learners and parents can subscribe for $4 a month or $44 a year, and school districts can license it with additional controls.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Adobe Firefly is Adobe's family of generative AI models designed specifically for creative professionals who need commercially-safe imagery. Unlike competitors trained on scraped internet images, Firefly is trained exclusively on Adobe Stock, openly licensed content, and public domain material—making it uniquely safe for commercial use without IP concerns. Firefly powers AI features across the Creative Cloud including Generative Fill and Generative Expand in Photoshop, Text to Vector Graphic in Illustrator, and the standalone Firefly web app. With the Firefly Image 3 model (late 2024), Adobe achieved near-parity with competitors on image quality while maintaining its commercial-safety advantage. For enterprise teams, Adobe offers indemnification against IP claims—a significant differentiator for agencies and brands.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>AI Photo Editor is a browser-based image editing platform built on Google's Gemini 3 Pro model, offered in a version the site calls "Nano Banana Pro." It covers a range of edits from a single interface: background removal, object removal via a Magic Eraser tool, upscaling to HD, style transformation and AI filters, batch processing of multiple images, text editing and translation within images, hairstyle changes, and sketch-to-photo conversion. The site states it is used in 120+ countries and reports 250,000+ images processed and a 4.7/5 rating; these figures are self-reported by the site rather than drawn from an independent review platform.</p>
</div>

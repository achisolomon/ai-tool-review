## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Krea AI is a real-time AI image generation platform that stands out for its instant visual feedback—you see images form as you type or sketch. Unlike traditional AI image generators that require waiting for results, Krea's real-time canvas lets creators iterate at the speed of thought. The platform aggregates access to cutting-edge models including Flux, Stable Diffusion XL, and proprietary fine-tunes, making it a one-stop shop for AI image creation. Beyond generation, Krea offers powerful enhancement tools including AI upscaling up to 4K, background removal, and image-to-image transformation. The intuitive web interface appeals to both beginners exploring AI art and professionals integrating AI into creative workflows.</p>
</div>

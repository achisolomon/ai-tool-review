## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Flux2Pro (the site calls itself "Flux 2") is a browser image generator for text-to-image and image editing with style transfer. Besides a model it calls Flux 2, it lists Nano Banana 2 and Pro, GPT Image 2 and 2.5, and Seedream 5.0, with outputs up to 4K in PNG, JPG or WebP and several aspect ratios. It is not Black Forest Labs' official FLUX.2 service: the site is independent of BFL, names no operator, and its "12B parameter" claim does not match BFL's flagship FLUX.2 model, which secondary sources put at 32B. For the official models, see <a href="/tools/flux-black-forest-labs/">FLUX by Black Forest Labs</a>.</p>
</div>

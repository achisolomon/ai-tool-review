## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Melhorar Imagem ("Improve Image" in Portuguese) is a browser-based AI photo enhancement tool aimed at Portuguese-speaking users. It automatically improves clarity, sharpness, color balance, and lighting in uploaded photos, and includes unblurring, upscaling (up to 8x), noise and grain reduction for low-light shots, and automated white balance correction. The tool also offers short video enhancement previews. No installation is required; the site presents itself as a general-purpose photo-fixing tool for e-commerce sellers, real estate agents, social media managers, and casual users rather than a professional retouching suite.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>face rating is a facial analysis platform that uses 478-point face mesh mapping to score individual features (eyes, nose, jawline, forehead), symmetry, and proportions. It generates a prioritized action plan of improvement recommendations rated by effort and impact, along with style previews for hairstyles and makeup. Optional AI transformation previews are available, covering a glow-up look, a procedure preview, a hairstyle pack, and an age simulation. The site also offers free standalone tools: a face shape detector, an attractiveness test, and a golden ratio calculator.</p>
</div>

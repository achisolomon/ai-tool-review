## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Flux3 is a browser-based AI studio for making images and short videos. It offers four workflows: text-to-image, reference remix (up to four reference images), text-to-video and image-to-video, with controls for aspect ratio, style, resolution and duration. You sign in, generate, and pay with credits that are checked on the server. Flux3 states that it is an independent interface and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Black Forest Labs, the maker of the FLUX models, and it does not say which models it runs on.</p>
</div>

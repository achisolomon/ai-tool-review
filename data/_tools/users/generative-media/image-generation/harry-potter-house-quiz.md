## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Harry Potter House Quiz is an unofficial, fan-made website that sorts you into Gryffindor, Ravenclaw, Hufflepuff, or Slytherin from a 2-5 minute quiz of 20+ questions, with no registration. On top of the quiz it adds three AI features: a personalized Hogwarts acceptance letter, an AI-generated portrait of you in your house robes, and Summon Patronus, which combines your photo with your patronus in a magical scene. The site also hosts wand, patronus, character, and Quidditch quizzes in 11 languages. It is not affiliated with Warner Bros. or J.K. Rowling.</p>
</div>

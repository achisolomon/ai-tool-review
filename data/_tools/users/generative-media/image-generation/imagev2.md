## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>ImageV2 is a browser image generator and editor that puts several third-party models behind one credit balance: GPT Images 2.0 and 2.5, Nano Banana 2 and 2.1, and the Seedream 5 family. It handles text-to-image, image-to-image and reference-based editing, taking up to 16 reference images with GPT Images 2.0 and 14 with Nano Banana 2 and Seedream 5 Lite, at 1K, 2K or 4K. It also lists a background remover and an upscaler. ImageV2 is independent of OpenAI, Google and ByteDance and does not name its operator.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Ideogram is an AI image generation platform founded by former Google Brain researchers, specifically designed to excel at rendering accurate, readable text within images. While competitors struggle with typography, Ideogram consistently produces clean logos, posters, signs, and branded content with legible text. The platform offers Ideogram 3.0, their latest model featuring enhanced photorealism, improved prompt adherence, and industry-leading text generation. With a generous free tier and intuitive interface, Ideogram has become the go-to tool for designers, marketers, and creators who need text-heavy visuals without the typical AI text garbling.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Midjourney is the aesthetic benchmark in AI image generation, created by an independent research lab led by David Holz (co-founder of Leap Motion). Unlike competitors focused on photorealism or speed, Midjourney prioritizes artistic quality and visual appeal, producing images with a distinctive cinematic look. The V6.1 model delivers unprecedented detail, coherent text rendering, and natural human features. Operating primarily through Discord with a newer web interface, Midjourney has built one of the largest creative AI communities, where millions of artists, designers, and hobbyists generate everything from concept art to architectural visualizations.</p>
</div>

    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>

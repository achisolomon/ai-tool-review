## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Recraft is a professional AI image generation platform that stands out for its unique ability to create both raster images and true vector graphics (SVG). Founded in 2023 by a team of ex-Yandex engineers, Recraft's V3 model achieved the #1 ranking on the Artificial Analysis text-to-image leaderboard, outperforming DALL-E 3, Midjourney, and Stable Diffusion. The platform is specifically designed for designers and brand teams who need consistent visual styles across projects. Unlike competitors focused solely on artistic generation, Recraft emphasizes practical design workflows with features like infinite canvas, brand color palettes, custom style training, and export-ready vector output for logos and illustrations.</p>
</div>

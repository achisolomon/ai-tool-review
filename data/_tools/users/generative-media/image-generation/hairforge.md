## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>hairforge lets users preview different beard styles on their own photo and get personalized recommendations based on their facial characteristics. It detects face shape (round, oval, square, diamond, oblong, heart) and analyzes beard density to match styles from light stubble to full beards. Generated previews are shown from multiple angles, front, left 45°, and right 45°, so users can see how a style would look from more than one side before deciding.</p>
</div>

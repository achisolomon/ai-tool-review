## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Nano Banana is Google's AI image generator embedded directly into the Gemini ecosystem. Launched in August 2025 and powered by Gemini 3 Pro and Gemini 3.1 Flash Image models, it takes a conversational approach to image creation—you describe what you want, upload reference images, and iteratively refine through follow-up prompts. Unlike standalone generators, Nano Banana understands context from your conversation and can maintain consistency across multiple edits. The premium Nano Banana Pro model delivers higher precision, more detail, and nuanced outputs.</p>
</div>

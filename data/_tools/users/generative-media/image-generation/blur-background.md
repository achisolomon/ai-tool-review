## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Blur Background, branded Vignra, is a web-based photo editing tool that blurs the background of a photo while keeping the subject in focus. The free Basic tier processes images locally in the browser, with no upload to a server and no AI involved, just an adjustable local blur with a real-time preview and before/after comparison. A separate "AI Refined" mode uses paid credits to apply AI-based bokeh for a more natural depth-of-field effect. The tool supports JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, and TIFF files up to 20MB.</p>
</div>

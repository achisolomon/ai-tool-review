## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Leonardo.AI is an AI-powered creative platform that has quickly become the go-to tool for game developers, concept artists, and digital creators. Founded in Australia in 2022, Leonardo distinguishes itself through its focus on production-ready assets rather than just artistic imagery. The platform offers multiple AI models including their flagship Phoenix model optimized for prompt adherence and photorealism, plus specialized models for anime, 3D renders, and illustration styles. Unlike competitors, Leonardo provides a real-time canvas for live AI generation, motion features for animating images, and robust training capabilities to create custom models from your own assets. With a generous free tier offering 150 daily tokens and API access for developers, Leonardo has attracted over 19 million users who generate everything from game sprites to marketing visuals.</p>
</div>

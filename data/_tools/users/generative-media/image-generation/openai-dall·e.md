## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>DALL-E is OpenAI's pioneering AI image generation system, now in its third generation. Unlike standalone image generators, DALL-E 3 is deeply integrated into ChatGPT, allowing users to generate images through natural conversation. The system is renowned for its exceptional prompt adherence—understanding and accurately rendering complex, detailed descriptions including text, spatial relationships, and specific styles. DALL-E 3 rewrites user prompts behind the scenes using GPT-4 to maximize output quality, making it the most accessible high-quality image generator for non-technical users. With built-in safety guardrails and clear commercial usage rights, it's become the go-to choice for businesses and creators needing reliable, legally safe AI imagery.</p>
</div>

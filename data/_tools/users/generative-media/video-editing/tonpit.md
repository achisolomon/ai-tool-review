## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Tonpit is a browser video studio that puts AI footage, AI images, voiceover, captions, a music library and a timeline editor in one place. You can build a video yourself, or connect your own AI agent (Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client) and let it assemble animations, cut your footage and rebuild templates around your product, photos or story. A built-in assistant covers people who do not bring an agent.</p>
<p>The cheapest paid plan is aimed at the agent workflow: $25 a month gives unlimited exports, and your agent's own thinking does not use Tonpit credits. Higher plans add monthly AI credits for generated clips, images and voiceover. Exports are MP4 or ProRes. Tonpit's roadmap names Seedance, Kling, MiniMax, FLUX, Seedream and Gemini as the generation models behind it.</p>
</div>

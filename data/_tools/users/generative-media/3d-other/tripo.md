## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Tripo AI is a fast AI-powered 3D generation platform that transforms text descriptions or single images into production-ready 3D models within seconds. Built on proprietary large-scale 3D foundation models, Tripo generates high-quality meshes with PBR textures suitable for games, animation, AR/VR, and 3D printing. The platform offers both a web interface for casual users and a robust API for developers integrating 3D generation into their pipelines. Tripo stands out for its speed - generating draft models in under 10 seconds - while maintaining topology suitable for rigging and animation.</p>
</div>

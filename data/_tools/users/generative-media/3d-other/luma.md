## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Luma AI is a pioneering 3D AI company that combines neural radiance field (NeRF) technology with generative AI to create the most accessible 3D capture and generation tools available. Founded in 2021 by Amit Jain and others, Luma made 3D scanning accessible to anyone with an iPhone through their breakthrough NeRF-based capture app. Their Genie product brings text-to-3D and image-to-3D generation, allowing creators to generate game-ready 3D assets from simple prompts or reference images in seconds. Whether you need to capture real-world objects with photorealistic fidelity or generate entirely new 3D models from imagination, Luma AI delivers professional-quality results with consumer-friendly tools.</p>
</div>

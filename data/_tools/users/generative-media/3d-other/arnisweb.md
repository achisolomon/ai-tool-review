## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>ArnisWeb is a browser-based generator that turns real-world locations into playable Minecraft worlds. You search for a city, address or landmark, draw a rectangle on the map, pick the edition and settings, and the service builds the world automatically from OpenStreetMap features (buildings, roads, water, nature) on real elevation terrain. Java worlds download as a ZIP world folder, and Bedrock worlds as .mcworld where offered. It is a hosted service built on the open-source Arnis project, so there is nothing to install. It is not an official Minecraft product and is not associated with Mojang or Microsoft.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use ArnisWeb?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Minecraft players who want to explore their own city or famous places</li>
        <li>Teachers using Minecraft for geography and urban planning lessons</li>
        <li>Server owners and creators who need a realistic map fast</li>
        <li>Anyone who wants Arnis without installing the desktop app</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Detailed, hand-accurate building interiors</li>
        <li>Very large regions (maps are capped at 50 km²)</li>
        <li>Users comfortable running the free open-source Arnis app locally</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Real places to a playable world in a few clicks, entirely in the browser</li>
      <li>Supports Java and Bedrock editions</li>
      <li>Built on Arnis, a popular, actively maintained open-source project</li>
      <li>Free generator to try, plus ready-made city maps</li>
      <li>Control over game mode, time of day and spawn point</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://arnisweb.com/" target="_blank">ArnisWeb</a> · <a href="https://github.com/louis-e/arnis" target="_blank">Arnis on GitHub</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Points-based pricing: 45 points per km², 50-point minimum per map</li>
      <li>Yearly plan points expire at the end of each billing period</li>
      <li>Building detail depends on how well OpenStreetMap covers the area</li>
      <li>Generation time is not stated and depends on area size and load</li>
      <li>The operator behind the hosted service is not named</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://arnisweb.com/#plans" target="_blank">ArnisWeb plans</a> · <a href="https://arnisweb.com/about" target="_blank">About</a></div>

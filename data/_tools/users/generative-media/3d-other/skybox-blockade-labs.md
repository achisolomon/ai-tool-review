## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Skybox AI by Blockade Labs is the leading AI tool for generating 360-degree panoramic environments from text descriptions. Unlike traditional skybox creation that requires expensive photography equipment or skilled 3D artists, Skybox AI lets anyone create immersive HDR environments in seconds. Simply describe your scene—"ancient forest at sunset" or "cyberpunk city street in rain"—and the AI generates a seamless, high-quality 360 panorama ready for use in Unity, Unreal Engine, VR applications, or architectural renders. With over 30 style presets ranging from realistic to anime, and export options including equirectangular, cubemap, and depth maps, Skybox AI has become an essential tool for indie game developers, VR creators, and visualization professionals seeking fast, affordable environment assets.</p>
</div>

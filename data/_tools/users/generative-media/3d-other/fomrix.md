## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Fomrix is a web platform that transforms images or text descriptions into 3D models. It covers image-to-3D conversion with textures, text-to-3D generation, image-to-STL conversion for 3D printing, and a 3D character maker, using the site's own "H3.1" and "P1" AI models. Generated models can be viewed in real time with 360-degree orbit controls and adjustable lighting, and exported as GLB, STL, or OBJ. The site also includes browser-based tools for viewing, converting, and repairing 3D files without uploading them elsewhere.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Meshy is an AI-powered 3D content generation platform that transforms text descriptions or 2D images into fully textured, production-ready 3D models. Built for game developers, digital artists, architects, and 3D hobbyists, Meshy dramatically accelerates the 3D asset creation pipeline that traditionally takes hours or days down to minutes. The platform supports multiple generation modes including Text-to-3D, Image-to-3D, and AI Texturing for existing meshes, with exports to industry-standard formats like FBX, OBJ, GLTF, and USDZ.</p>
</div>

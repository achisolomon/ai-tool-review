## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>ElevenLabs is the leading AI voice synthesis platform, renowned for producing the most natural and emotionally expressive synthetic speech available. Founded in 2022, it offers text-to-speech, instant voice cloning, professional voice cloning, and AI dubbing for video content. The platform supports 32+ languages with native-quality pronunciation and has become the go-to solution for content creators, podcasters, game developers, and enterprises needing high-quality voice generation at scale.</p>
</div>

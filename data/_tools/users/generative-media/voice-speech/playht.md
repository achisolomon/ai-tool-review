## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p><strong>Note: PlayHT was acquired by Meta in July 2025 and permanently shut down on December 31, 2025. This page is preserved for historical reference.</strong></p>

<p>PlayHT (later rebranded PlayAI) was a text-to-speech and voice cloning platform founded in 2020 by Mahmoud Felfel and Hammad Syed. The platform specialized in making AI-generated speech virtually indistinguishable from human recordings, offering 900+ voices across 142 languages with instant voice cloning from just 3 seconds of audio. PlayHT served enterprise clients including Amazon, Samsung, Xerox, Walgreens, Salesforce, RedBull, and Volvo before Meta acquired the entire 35-person team to power Meta AI, AI Characters, and wearable products.</p>
</div>

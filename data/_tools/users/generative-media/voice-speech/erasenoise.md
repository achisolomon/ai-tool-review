## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>EraseNoise is a browser-based tool that removes constant background noise, such as wind, fans, traffic and electrical hum, from speech in audio and video files. Upload an MP3, WAV, M4A or other audio file, or an MP4, MOV or WebM video up to 2 GB, listen to a free 30-second preview, and download the cleaned result. Video keeps its original picture: H.264 and HEVC footage is copied rather than re-encoded, and only the soundtrack changes.</p>
<p>Beyond noise removal, the site bundles echo and reverb removal, an AI audio enhancer, silence removal, volume normalization and vocal separation. Uploads and results are deleted automatically 24 hours after upload. The site does not name the company behind it or the model it uses.</p>
</div>

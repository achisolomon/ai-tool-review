## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Lyric Video Maker turns an uploaded audio track into a finished lyric video. It automatically transcribes the lyrics (50+ languages supported), times them to the beat, and generates mood-driven background visuals, so users don't need to type out lyrics or manually sync captions. The transcription can be edited before the final render. Output is available in multiple aspect ratios (16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok/Reels, 1:1 for feed posts), and exported files are compatible with Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, and Final Cut Pro for further editing.</p>
</div>

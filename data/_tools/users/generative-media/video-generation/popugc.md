## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>PopUGC is a generation studio aimed at e-commerce sellers who need product images and marketing videos without hiring a studio or filming UGC-style ads. It covers text-to-image, image-to-image, text-to-video, and image-to-video generation, and supports batch creation for sellers who need to produce many variations at once. The tool runs on premium third-party models, including Veo 3.1 and Nano Banana 2, rather than a proprietary model, and downloads come without a watermark on all paid plans.</p>
</div>

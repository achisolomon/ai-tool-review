## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Kling AI is the video generation platform from Kuaishou Technology, China's second-largest short video company. Launched in June 2024, Kling quickly became a serious competitor to Runway and Pika by offering exceptional quality at significantly lower costs. The Kling 2.0 model (released late 2024) delivers cinema-quality video generation with realistic physics, coherent motion, and up to 3-minute durations—far exceeding competitors' typical 5-10 second limits. Kling's key differentiator is value: users consistently get more high-quality generations per dollar compared to Western alternatives, making it the go-to choice for creators who need volume without sacrificing quality.</p>
</div>

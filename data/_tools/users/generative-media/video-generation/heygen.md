## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>HeyGen is an AI-powered video creation platform that transforms text into professional videos featuring realistic AI avatars. The platform offers 200+ stock avatars, custom avatar creation from a single photo or video, and voice cloning that captures your unique speaking style. Its standout feature is Video Translate, which automatically dubs and lip-syncs videos into 175+ languages while preserving the speaker's natural voice. Used by enterprises like Salesforce, Amazon, and Accenture, HeyGen has become the go-to solution for training videos, marketing content, and global video localization.</p>
</div>

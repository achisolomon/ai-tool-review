## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Visemix creates lip-sync videos by matching mouth movement to a new audio track on an uploaded video or photo, for clips up to 120 seconds. Audio can come from an uploaded file, a browser recording, or text-to-speech. The tool uses the "MiniMax H3 Max" model, which is specialized for turning a static talking photo into a synced video, and offers sample avatars for users without their own footage. Files are pre-checked locally in the browser before upload, and finished videos download without a watermark.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>BabyVideo.ai generates "future baby" preview images by analyzing one or two uploaded parent photos and blending facial features such as eye color, hair texture, skin tone, face shape, nose, lips, and cheek fullness. Users select the desired baby gender before generation. The tool can then convert the resulting image into a short video, with variations such as the baby talking, singing, or dancing. The site itself describes the output as entertainment, not a medical, genetic, or scientific prediction of a real child's appearance.</p>
</div>

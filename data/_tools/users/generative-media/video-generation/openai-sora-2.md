## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>OpenAI Sora 2 is OpenAI's flagship AI video generation model, representing a major leap in text-to-video technology. Originally previewed in early 2024 and fully launched in December 2024, Sora generates highly realistic and imaginative video scenes from text descriptions. What sets Sora apart is its deep understanding of physics, motion, and real-world dynamics—characters walk naturally, objects interact believably, and camera movements feel cinematic. Sora 2 introduced improved coherence, longer durations up to 20 seconds, and new features like Storyboard mode for multi-shot narratives, Remix for video transformation, and Re-cut for video editing. Available through ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscriptions, Sora has become the benchmark for AI video quality and narrative capability.</p>
</div>

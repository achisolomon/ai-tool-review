## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Synthesia is the leading AI video platform for creating professional business videos using lifelike AI avatars and text-to-speech voiceovers. Founded in 2017 by researchers from UCL, Stanford, TUM, and Cambridge, it enables anyone to create studio-quality videos by simply typing a script—no cameras, actors, or video editing skills required. With 230+ diverse AI avatars speaking 140+ languages, Synthesia has become the go-to solution for enterprise training, onboarding, and internal communications at scale. Over 50% of Fortune 100 companies use Synthesia for their video content needs.</p>
</div>

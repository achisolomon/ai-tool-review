## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Pika is an AI video generation platform founded by Stanford PhD researchers Demi Guo and Chenlin Meng in 2023. Starting as a viral Discord bot, Pika has evolved into a full web platform known for its creative approach to video generation. Unlike competitors focused purely on realism, Pika excels at stylized, artistic video generation with unique features like Pikaframes (animated photo effects), Scene Ingredients (control composition elements), and viral "Pikaffects" that turn photos into creative transformations (inflate, melt, explode, crush effects). Pika 2.1 delivers improved motion quality and longer clips, making it popular for social media content, artistic projects, and creative experimentation.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Video Watermark Remover is a browser-based tool for removing watermarks, logos, and text overlays from video files. It uses CNN and GAN neural networks to detect and remove marks frame by frame, with processing taking roughly 2 to 5 minutes per video. The site targets two main use cases: clips downloaded from TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram, and AI-generated videos from tools like Sora 2, Pika, and Runway that carry a model watermark. Uploaded videos are encrypted and automatically deleted after processing, and the tool supports MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WMV, FLV, and 3GP formats.</p>
</div>

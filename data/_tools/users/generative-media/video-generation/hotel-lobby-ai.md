## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Hotel Lobby AI is a niche video generator built around a single viral template: the orange-booth, single-mic rap duet popularized by Quavo and Takeoff's 2022 "Hotel Lobby" performance for A COLORS SHOW. Users upload one clear, front-facing photo per performer, pick a stage (Hotel Lobby, Luxury Lobby, Studio Booth, or Street Cypher), choose length, resolution, and aspect ratio, and the tool generates a video with both subjects lip-synced and animated to an original, AI-synthesized rap track. The booth, hanging microphone, camera framing, and audio are generated fresh for each clip rather than pulled from a reference track, and the tool accepts faces, illustrated characters, and animals as performers.</p>
</div>

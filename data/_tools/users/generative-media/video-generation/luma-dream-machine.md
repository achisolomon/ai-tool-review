## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Luma Dream Machine is an AI video generation platform developed by Luma Labs, powered by the Ray3 model family that produces photorealistic video with coherent motion and native 1080p/HDR output. Originally launched in June 2024, Dream Machine excels at image-to-video generation, delivering some of the smoothest and most natural-feeling motion in the AI video space. The platform supports text-to-video, image-to-video, and video transformation with character references, accessible through both a browser-based workspace and iOS application. Note: Luma Labs has indicated that "Dream Machine" is transitioning to the broader "Luma" brand name.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Runway is the pioneering AI video generation platform that brought text-to-video to the mainstream. Co-founded by Cristobal Valenzuela in 2018, Runway has evolved from a research tool into the most feature-rich creative AI platform for filmmakers, content creators, and studios. With Gen-4.5 (their latest model), Runway delivers cinematic-quality video generation with unprecedented control over motion, camera movement, and visual style. The platform combines text-to-video, image-to-video, Motion Brush for precise animation control, and a full suite of editing tools—making it the go-to choice for professionals who need more than just video generation.</p>
</div>

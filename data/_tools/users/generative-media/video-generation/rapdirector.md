## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>RapDirector is an AI video tool built for rappers: upload one clear front-facing photo and your own rap track, pick a 10-second verse or hook, choose a delivery style (Confident, High energy, or Laid-back), and it renders a 720p lip-sync performance clip using your original audio. Beyond single-performer videos it offers AI Rap Duo for two-performer exchanges, animated rap video concepts, and general AI image and video workspaces. It targets independent artists and creators making release teasers and social content.</p>
</div>

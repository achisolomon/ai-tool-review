## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>SceneFlare is a browser platform for AI images, short videos, visual effects and photo edits. You pick a model, quality and aspect ratio, and it shows the credit cost before you generate. Video models include Kling 4.0, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.5, Vidu Q4, Wan 3.0 and MiniMax H3; image models include GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2.1 and Seedream 5.0. Editing tools cover background, object and person removal, upscaling, text edits and a hairstyle changer. SceneFlare is an independent reseller of these models: it says they come from "different providers" and claims no partnership with their makers.</p>
</div>

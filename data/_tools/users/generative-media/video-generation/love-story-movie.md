## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Love Story Movie turns a couple's photos and memories into a short private film. You write 5 to 10 memories, pair each with photos (used to keep faces, hair, glasses and clothing recognizable), and review the people, scenes and closing message in a free preview before paying. After a one-time $99 payment it generates a 60 to 110 second HD movie, usually within about 30 minutes, with a private viewing page and an MP4 download. AI-created scenes are described as "artistic interpretations"; the site does not name the models it uses.</p>
</div>

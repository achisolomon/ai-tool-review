## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Gemini Video is the video generation feature inside Google's Gemini app. You describe a scene in plain language, or upload up to five images, and Gemini returns a 10-second clip with sound generated alongside the picture. You then change it by chatting ("make it night", "move the camera left") instead of starting over. Since Google I/O in May 2026 it runs on Gemini Omni Flash, which replaced Veo 3.1 in the app; the August 2026 Omni 1.1 update added scene extension up to 40 seconds. The same model also powers Google Flow, Google Vids and YouTube Shorts. Inside the Gemini app it needs a Google AI subscription; without one, the free route is short clips in YouTube Shorts and YouTube Create.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use Gemini Video?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Short social clips, ads and Shorts with sound included</li>
        <li>People already paying for Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra</li>
        <li>Product shots and photos turned into moving scenes</li>
        <li>Iterating by chat instead of learning a video editor</li>
        <li>Students (free Omni access for a year, limited offer)</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Free users who want it inside the Gemini app</li>
        <li>Long or multi-shot stories (10s per clip, 40s max with extensions)</li>
        <li>Volume work: testers hit the limit after 3-5 videos</li>
        <li>Content with celebrities, brands or copyrighted characters (frequent refusals)</li>
        <li>Under-18s, and video-upload editing in the EEA, UK and Switzerland</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Audio (dialogue, ambient sound, effects) generated with the video, no separate tool</li>
      <li>Chat-based editing keeps the scene and changes only what you ask; testers call it the standout feature</li>
      <li>"Very professional" output quality for short clips</li>
      <li>Usually renders in under a minute</li>
      <li>Ranks above Veo 3.1 and Kling 3.0 on the Artificial Analysis video arena</li>
      <li>Image-to-video from up to five references; uploaded product details stay intact</li>
      <li>First/last-frame control and 4K upscaling (Omni 1.1, in Flow and the API)</li>
      <li>Free short clips in YouTube Shorts and YouTube Create (18+)</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>No video generation in the Gemini app on the free plan</li>

      <li>Some jobs reported stuck pending for hours before a retry worked</li>
      <li>Native output is 720p; 1080p and 4K are upscaled</li>
      <li>Visible watermark overlay; in India, South Korea and Vietnam only AI Ultra can turn it off</li>

      <li>Scene extension in 10s steps up to 40s (also in the Gemini app)</li>
      <li>First/last-frame interpolation for orbits, zooms and loops (Flow, API)</li>
      <li>360p drafts: up to 60% faster at a third of the 720p cost (API)</li>
      <li>Edit an uploaded video (10s max) plus up to five images</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="detail-section">
    <h4>Limits</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Usage refreshes every 5 hours until a weekly cap</li>
      <li>Video "requires the most usage" of any Gemini feature</li>
      <li>Plus 2x, Pro 4x, Ultra 5x or 20x of Pro limits</li>
      <li>Output 720p native; 1080p and 4K upscaled</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="detail-section">
    <h4>Safety & Availability</h4>
    <ul>

      <li>Visible watermark overlay, controlled under Settings &gt; Media Watermark</li>
      <li>18+ only; Omni available to subscribers globally</li>
      <li>Video-upload editing not available in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states</li>

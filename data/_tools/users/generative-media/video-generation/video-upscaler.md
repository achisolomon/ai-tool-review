## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Video Upscaler is an AI platform for enhancing video quality through resolution upscaling (2x, 4x, HD, 2K, 4K) and reducing blur, noise, and compression artifacts. It runs on Topaz Proteus technology for frame analysis. Listed use cases include restoring old footage, optimizing clips for social media, and upscaling anime video. It accepts mp4, mov, webm, m4v, and gif files up to 200MB and 60 seconds in length.</p>
</div>

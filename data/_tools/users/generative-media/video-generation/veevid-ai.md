## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Veevid AI is a video generation platform that turns text prompts or images into videos with synchronized audio. Instead of building its own model, it gives users access to multiple third-party AI video models, including Kling 4.0, LTX 2.5, and Grok, from a single interface. It also includes video upscaling and extension tools, and can output video at up to 4K resolution.</p>
</div>

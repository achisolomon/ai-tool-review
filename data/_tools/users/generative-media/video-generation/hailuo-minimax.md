## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Hailuo AI is an AI video generation platform developed by MiniMax, a Shanghai-based AI company backed by Alibaba and Tencent. The platform converts text prompts and images into short video clips up to 10 seconds in 1080p resolution. Hailuo 2.3 features Director Mode for cinematic camera control, subject reference for character consistency across clips, start/end frame conditioning, and enhanced support for anime, illustration, and stylized art like ink wash painting. The model ranks #1 for physics simulation on WorldModelBench and generates videos in 30-90 seconds, making it one of the fastest in its category. MiniMax went public on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in January 2026 at a $4 billion valuation.</p>
</div>

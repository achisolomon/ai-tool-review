## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>creatview is an AI video generation workspace that turns a concept, script, image, or product URL into a finished multi-shot video. Users describe an idea in natural language, and the system automatically selects which underlying AI model to use, routing between models such as Sora 2, Veo 3.1, and Seedance 2.0 depending on the shot, then plans scenes and generates an editable video. It can also analyze a reference video URL to study its pacing and rebuild a similar structure around the user's own content. A product integration feature converts an Amazon or Shopify product URL directly into a marketing video. Editing happens through a chat interface, and finished videos export automatically into multiple formats for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and ad placements, with voiceover and captions available in 30+ languages. The platform includes 25+ pre-built templates covering product ads, UGC-style content, testimonials, explainers, and cinematic styles.</p>
</div>

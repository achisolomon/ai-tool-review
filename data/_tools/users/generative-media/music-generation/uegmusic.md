## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>UEGMusic creates short audio identities: spoken jingles, producer tags, DJ drops, radio station IDs, podcast intros, and sung brand jingles. It combines a voice (AI-generated, uploaded, or recorded) with background music and sound effects, drawing on a library of 100+ background tracks. Mix controls cover volume, timing, echo, and fade, and separate audio editing tools (trimmer, joiner, normalizer, fade) are included for refining the result. An "agent mode" lets users describe their audio concept conversationally instead of configuring each setting manually.</p>
</div>

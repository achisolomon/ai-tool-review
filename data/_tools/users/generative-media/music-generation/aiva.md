## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>AIVA (Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist) is a pioneering AI music composition platform that specializes in creating emotional soundtracks for films, video games, commercials, and other media. Unlike song-generation tools like Suno that focus on pop music with vocals, AIVA excels at orchestral, cinematic, and instrumental compositions. In 2016, AIVA made history by becoming the first AI composer to be officially registered with SACEM, the French professional association of composers. The platform offers unparalleled control through features like stem downloads, MIDI export, and the ability to influence compositions via style presets, custom uploads, and chord progressions. For creators who need professional-quality scores with clear copyright ownership, AIVA provides a unique combination of AI generation power and traditional composition flexibility.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>AISongsGenerator creates music from text prompts or user-written lyrics. An AI lyrics generator can write lyrics to a specified theme, mood, structure, and style before the song is generated. The platform also includes audio tools beyond generation: a vocal remover, a stem splitter, and a cover-generation feature that re-styles existing songs. It can produce an instrumental-only version with no lead vocals, and it can export a music video (MP4) built around the generated track.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Ironclad is a contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform used by legal, procurement and sales teams to run contracts from request to signature to renewal. Its building blocks are a no-code workflow designer, a collaborative editor, a searchable contract repository and reporting, plus Clickwrap for online agreements. Ironclad AI layers on top: it reviews third-party paper against your playbook, redlines, extracts key terms into the repository, and answers questions about your contract history, which Ironclad describes as agentic contracting grounded in your own deal history.</p>
<p>Founded in San Francisco in 2014 by Jason Boehmig and Cai GoGwilt, Ironclad raised a $150M Series E at a $3.2B valuation in 2022 and counts customers such as OpenAI, L'Oréal, Mastercard and Snap. It is named a Leader in both the Gartner Magic Quadrant and the Forrester Wave for CLM (2025).</p>
</div>

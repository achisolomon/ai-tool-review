## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Spellbook is an AI assistant for contract lawyers that works inside Microsoft Word. It reviews agreements clause by clause, flags risky or missing terms, suggests redlines in track changes, compares a draft against market standards, and drafts new clauses or whole documents from instructions. Because it sits in Word, lawyers don't have to move documents into another platform. Firms can add their own playbooks and precedents so suggestions match their positions.</p>
<p>The Toronto company reports nearly 4,000 law firms and in-house legal teams as customers. It raised a $50M Series B in October 2025 led by Keith Rabois at Khosla Ventures, then $40M in debt financing from RBCx in March 2026 to fund acquisitions, and has an exclusive deal with the Canadian Bar Association.</p>
</div>

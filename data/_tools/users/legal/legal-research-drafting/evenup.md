## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>EvenUp is AI built only for personal injury law. Its best-known product drafts demand letters: it reads a client's medical records and bills, builds the treatment chronology, calculates damages and produces a demand with line-level citations to the records, in minutes instead of days. The platform has since expanded across the case lifecycle, from intake and treatment tracking to discovery, trial preparation and case analytics.</p>
<p>Its Piai model is trained on more than 250,000 verdicts and settlements, which EvenUp uses to suggest case values. Founded in San Francisco in 2019, EvenUp raised a $150M Series E at a $2B valuation in October 2025, led by Bessemer, and says it processes about 10,000 cases a week. Drafts go through in-house human review before delivery.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Nabla is an ambient AI assistant for clinicians. It listens to the patient encounter, in person or via telehealth, and drafts a structured note in the clinician's preferred format within seconds, along with patient instructions and referral letters. Nabla emphasizes privacy (audio isn't stored by default) and fits into existing EHRs including Epic, athenahealth, Oracle Health and NextGen, as well as a browser extension for others.</p>
<p>Nabla was founded in Paris in 2018 by Alexandre Lebrun, Delphine Groll and Martin Raison. It has raised more than $120M, including a $70M Series C in 2025 led by HV Capital, and reports more than 85,000 clinicians across 150+ health organizations. Unlike most competitors, it offers a free tier and individual plans as well as enterprise deployments.</p>
</div>

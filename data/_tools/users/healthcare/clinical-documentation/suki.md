## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Suki is an AI assistant for doctors and other clinicians. Like other ambient scribes, Suki Assistant listens to the patient visit and drafts a structured note, but it goes further: clinicians can use voice commands to navigate the EHR and update records, get ICD-10 and HCC coding suggestions, ask clinical questions, and work in several languages. It integrates bidirectionally with Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth and MEDITECH.</p>
<p>Founded in 2017 by Punit Soni, Suki raised a $90M Series E in October 2026 to expand its agents into clinical, financial and operational workflows. A 2026 KLAS validation across FMOL Health, McLeod Health and Rush measured meaningful financial impact per provider. Pricing is quote-based.</p>
</div>

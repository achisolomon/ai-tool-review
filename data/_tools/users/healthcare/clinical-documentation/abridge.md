## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Abridge is an ambient AI documentation platform for clinicians. With the patient's consent, it listens to the visit on a phone or in the EHR, then drafts a structured clinical note in seconds, ready for the clinician to review and sign. A feature called Linked Evidence ties every sentence in the note back to the exact moment in the conversation it came from, which makes notes easier to verify and trust. It supports many specialties and languages, and generates patient-friendly visit summaries.</p>
<p>Abridge was founded in Pittsburgh in 2018 by cardiologist Shiv Rao. Its Epic integration is one of the deepest in the category, and it also works with other EHRs. It raised a $300M Series E led by Andreessen Horowitz in 2025 at a $5.3B valuation, crossed $100M in annual recurring revenue, and reports more than 300 health systems as customers, including Mayo Clinic, Duke, Johns Hopkins and Kaiser Permanente.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Hippocratic AI builds AI agents that talk directly with patients, mostly by phone. Health systems and payers use them for tasks that are important but hard to staff: pre-operative instructions, post-discharge follow-up calls, chronic disease check-ins, medication reminders, intake histories, screenings and appointment scheduling. The agents are designed to be warm and patient, speak several languages, and escalate to a human clinician when something needs attention.</p>
<p>Founded in 2023 by Munjal Shah, Hippocratic AI focuses heavily on safety. Its Polaris architecture uses a constellation of models that check each other's outputs, and in April 2026 it released Polaris 5.0. The company raised a $126M Series C at a $3.5B valuation led by Avenir, and works with more than 50 health systems and payers in six countries, including Cleveland Clinic, Northwestern Medicine and Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Trust.</p>
</div>

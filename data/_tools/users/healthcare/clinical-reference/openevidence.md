## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>OpenEvidence is an AI search and reference tool for doctors and other clinicians. You ask a clinical question in plain language (for example, about drug interactions, guideline recommendations or the evidence for a treatment) and it returns a concise answer with citations to peer-reviewed studies and guidelines. It has content agreements with major medical publishers, including the New England Journal of Medicine and JAMA, so answers can draw on full-text journal content. Newer features include deep-research style evidence reviews and help with documentation tasks.</p>
<p>OpenEvidence is free for verified US healthcare professionals and is funded by advertising, mostly from pharmaceutical companies. It raised a $250M Series D in January 2026 at a $12B valuation, led by Thrive Capital and DST, and reports that more than 40% of practicing US physicians use it daily.</p>
</div>

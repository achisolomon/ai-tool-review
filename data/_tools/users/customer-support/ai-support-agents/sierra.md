## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Sierra builds AI agents that large companies put in front of their customers on chat and phone. Each agent is designed to speak in the company's brand voice, follow its policies and take real actions in its systems, such as processing a return, troubleshooting a device or changing a subscription. Companies can build agents with Sierra's no-code tools or its Agent SDK, test them in simulation before launch, and monitor quality afterwards.</p>
<p>Sierra was founded in 2023 by Bret Taylor, former co-CEO of Salesforce and chair of OpenAI's board, and Clay Bavor, a longtime Google executive. It raised a $950M Series E in May 2026 at a $15.8B valuation, led by Tiger Global and GV. Sierra popularized outcome-based pricing: customers pay a pre-agreed rate when the agent resolves an issue without a human.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Fin is Intercom's AI agent for customer service. It answers customer questions from your help center, past conversations and other content, follows your written guidance on tone and policy, and can run workflows that take actions such as checking an order or updating an account. It works across chat, email and other channels, supports many languages, and hands off to a human when it should.</p>
<p>Fin's pricing is simple and public: $0.99 per outcome, where an outcome is a conversation the customer confirms is resolved, one where they don't ask for more help, or a completed workflow. It runs inside Intercom's own helpdesk (seat plans from $29/month) or on top of other helpdesks such as Salesforce with no seat fees, just a small monthly minimum of outcomes.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Decagon builds AI agents that handle customer conversations for large companies over chat, email and voice. Support teams describe how the agent should behave in natural-language Agent Operating Procedures (AOPs), which the platform turns into reliable workflows that can look up accounts, process refunds, change orders and escalate to humans. Analytics and testing tools show how conversations go, where customers get stuck and what to improve.</p>
<p>Founded in San Francisco in 2023 by Jesse Zhang and Ashwin Sreenivas, Decagon grew very quickly: it raised $250M at a $4.5B valuation in January 2026 and reports enterprise customers including Block, Chime, Notion, Duolingo and Avis Budget Group. Pricing is per conversation by default, with per-resolution pricing available.</p>
</div>

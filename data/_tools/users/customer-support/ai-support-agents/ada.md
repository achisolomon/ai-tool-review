## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Ada is one of the longest-running AI customer service companies. Its AI agents answer and resolve customer questions across web chat, email, voice, SMS and social messaging, connect to back-end systems to take actions such as checking an order or changing a booking, and hand off to human agents when needed. Ada runs alongside an existing helpdesk such as Zendesk or Salesforce rather than replacing it, and supports many languages.</p>
<p>Founded in Toronto in 2016 by Mike Murchison and David Hariri, Ada has raised more than $200M, including a $130M Series C in 2021 led by Spark Capital at a $1.2B valuation. It reports more than 6 billion customer interactions for brands such as Square, YETI, Pinterest, Canva and monday.com, and prices mainly on resolved conversations.</p>
</div>

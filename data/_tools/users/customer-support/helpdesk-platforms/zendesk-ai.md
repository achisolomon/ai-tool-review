## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Zendesk AI is the set of AI features built into Zendesk's customer service platform. It has three main parts: AI agents that answer and resolve customer conversations on their own across messaging, email and voice; Copilot, which helps human agents with suggested replies, summaries and next steps; and intelligent triage that detects intent, language and sentiment to route tickets. Everything shares the same tickets, knowledge base and reporting as the rest of Zendesk.</p>
<p>Pricing has three layers: Zendesk Suite seats (Team from $55 and Professional from $115 per agent per month, billed annually), the Copilot add-on at $50 per agent per month, and AI agents billed per automated resolution, about $1.50 committed or $2.00 pay-as-you-go. Since May 2026 Zendesk bills only verified resolutions, where the AI actually solved the issue.</p>
</div>

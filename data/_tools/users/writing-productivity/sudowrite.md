AI creative writing assistant for fiction authors

## Overview
Sudowrite is an AI-powered writing assistant specifically designed for fiction writers. It uses advanced language models to help with brainstorming, drafting, editing, and rewriting. The tool understands creative writing conventions and helps maintain narrative flow, character consistency, and dialogue quality.

## Key Features
- AI-powered story generation and brainstorming
- Scene and dialogue writing assistance
- Character development and consistency checks
- Narrative editing and pacing suggestions
- Inline AI editing with multiple suggestions
- Descriptive text enhancement
- Story structure guidance
- Integration with writing workflows

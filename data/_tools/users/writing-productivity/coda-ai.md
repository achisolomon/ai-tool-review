AI-native features within collaborative documents

## Overview
Coda AI is an integrated set of AI features built into Coda, a collaborative document platform. It provides AI blocks that can generate content, summarize information, extract data, and answer questions within the context of Coda documents. The AI integrates seamlessly with Coda's table and workflow features.

## Key Features
- Natural language question answering over document content
- AI-powered content generation and summarization
- Data extraction and transformation blocks
- Integration with Coda tables and databases
- Collaborative AI features for teams
- Formula and automation assistance

## Pricing
Coda AI is included in Coda Team and Business plans. Team plan starts at $150/month for the first 3 users. AI features available as paid add-on for free users at approximately $10/month per user.

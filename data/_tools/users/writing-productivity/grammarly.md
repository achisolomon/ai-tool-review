## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Grammarly is the leading AI-powered writing assistant used by 30+ million people daily across 500,000+ apps and websites. Founded in 2009, it has evolved from a basic grammar checker into a comprehensive communication platform with generative AI capabilities. The tool provides real-time suggestions for grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity, engagement, and tone—working seamlessly in browsers, desktop apps, email clients, and word processors. With GrammarlyGO (their generative AI feature), users can now compose, rewrite, and ideate content beyond just corrections. Enterprise teams at companies like Zoom, Cisco, and Expedia rely on Grammarly Business for brand-consistent communication.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use Grammarly?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Non-native English speakers and ESL learners</li>
        <li>Business professionals writing emails daily</li>
        <li>Students needing academic writing support</li>
        <li>Content creators focused on clarity and engagement</li>
        <li>Enterprise teams needing brand voice consistency</li>
        <li>Anyone wanting "works everywhere" writing help</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Creative writers (suggestions can feel formulaic)</li>
        <li>Technical documentation (misunderstands jargon)</li>
        <li>Heavy long-form content (use Notion AI or ChatGPT)</li>
        <li>Budget users (Premium required for full value)</li>
        <li>Privacy-concerned users (cloud-based processing)</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Works everywhere: 500K+ apps via browser extension, desktop, mobile</li>
      <li>Catches errors other tools miss—context-aware grammar (G2: 200+ mentions)</li>
      <li>Tone detection helps avoid miscommunication in emails</li>
      <li>Clean, non-intrusive inline suggestions</li>
      <li>Plagiarism checker included in Premium</li>
      <li>GrammarlyGO generates and rewrites content with AI</li>
      <li>Style guides for brand consistency (Business)</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://www.g2.com/products/grammarly/reviews" target="_blank">G2 Reviews</a> · <a href="https://www.grammarly.com" target="_blank">Official Site</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Free tier is very limited—Premium is the real product</li>
      <li>Can over-correct creative or stylistic writing (G2: 50+ mentions)</li>
      <li>GrammarlyGO has monthly prompt limits even on Premium</li>
      <li>Doesn't work well in some apps (Google Docs can be glitchy)</li>
      <li>Privacy concerns: text is processed on Grammarly servers</li>
      <li>Annual billing pushed heavily—monthly is expensive ($30/mo)</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://www.g2.com/products/grammarly/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons" target="_blank">G2 Pros & Cons</a> · <a href="https://www.capterra.com/p/135263/Grammarly/reviews/" target="_blank">Capterra</a></div>

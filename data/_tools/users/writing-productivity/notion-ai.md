## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Notion AI is an AI assistant built directly into Notion's workspace, turning your docs, tasks, and databases into an intelligent knowledge base. Unlike standalone AI tools, it works with context from your entire workspace—answering questions, writing drafts, summarizing meetings, and automating workflows. The new Notion Agent can complete multi-step tasks autonomously, while Custom Agents let teams automate recurring work on schedules or triggers.</p>
</div>

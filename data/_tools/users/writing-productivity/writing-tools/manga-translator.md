## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Manga Translator is an AI-powered translation tool built specifically for manga pages rather than general text or documents. It detects text through OCR, removes the original text, repairs the background art underneath it, and typesets the translated text back into the panel, preserving the page layout. It supports translation into 50 or more languages and offers a choice of translation models, including offline options, Sugoi, NLLB, M2M100, mBART, and ChatGPT. The tool also processes full PDF documents in parallel and handles manga-specific language quirks such as honorifics, catchphrases, and onomatopoeia rather than translating them literally. An optional offline deployment mode is available for users who want to keep pages off external servers.</p>

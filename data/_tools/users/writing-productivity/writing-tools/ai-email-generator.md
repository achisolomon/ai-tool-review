## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>AI Email Generator takes a short description of what a user wants to say and turns it into a drafted email with a subject line and a ready-to-edit message body. It includes a reply mode for responding to an existing email, with settings for tone, intent, and language. The same site also bundles a few adjacent utilities: a temporary email service, an email signature generator, and an email username generator. Basic use doesn't require creating an account.</p>
</div>

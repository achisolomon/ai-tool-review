## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>PDF Translator converts PDF documents between 60 or more languages directly in the browser, keeping the original formatting intact, including fonts, tables, images, and multi-column layouts. It handles scanned PDFs through OCR and supports files up to 200 MB. The free tier runs on Google Translate and Bing, while paid tiers add OpenAI, DeepL, and other engines. The tool also includes a document Q&A feature and can output a dual-column bilingual version alongside the translation.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Jasper (formerly Jarvis) is an AI marketing platform purpose-built for enterprise content teams. Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, Jasper focuses on marketing-specific use cases: ad copy, blog posts, social media, product descriptions, and email campaigns. Its Brand Voice feature learns your company's tone, style, and terminology to ensure all AI-generated content stays on-brand. With team collaboration, knowledge base integration, and workflow approval features, Jasper targets marketing teams at companies like Anthropic, HubSpot, and IBM who need consistent, scalable content production.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Copy.ai has evolved from a simple AI copywriting tool into a comprehensive Go-to-Market (GTM) AI platform. Founded in 2020, it now offers AI-powered workflows that automate entire sales and marketing processes, not just content creation. The platform includes 90+ templates for marketing copy, AI chatbots for customer engagement, sales automation tools, and workflow builders that connect multiple AI actions. Unlike single-purpose writing assistants, Copy.ai targets revenue teams who need to scale outreach, content production, and lead engagement simultaneously.</p>
</div>

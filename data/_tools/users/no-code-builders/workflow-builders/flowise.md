## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Flowise is an open-source agentic AI development platform that enables users to build AI agents and LLM-powered workflows through a visual drag-and-drop interface. Built on LangChain.js, it abstracts away the complexity of coding RAG pipelines, multi-agent systems, and chatbots. The platform offers three visual builder types: Assistant for beginners, Chatflow for single-agent systems, and Agentflow for complex multi-agent orchestration with human-in-the-loop capabilities. Flowise supports self-hosted deployments for full data sovereignty and was acquired by Workday in August 2025, bringing enterprise-grade stability to the platform.</p>
</div>

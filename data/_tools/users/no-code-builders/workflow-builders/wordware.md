## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Wordware started as a "prompt-first IDE": a Notion-like editor where you write instructions in plain English, add loops, branches and structured outputs, and deploy the result as an AI agent with its own API. It drew a large following in 2024, and the company raised a $30M seed round led by Spark Capital, which it describes as the largest seed in Y Combinator history.</p>
<p>In 2025 the company pivoted. Its main product is now Sauna, an AI workspace for knowledge workers that watches how you work, remembers past decisions and preferences, and after a task repeats a few times starts doing it for you and presents the result for review. The original Wordware builder (v1) is still reachable from the site, but new development is focused on Sauna, which has been in early access. Treat this listing as a company in transition.</p>
</div>

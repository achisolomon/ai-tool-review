## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Lindy lets you build AI agents ("Lindies") that do work rather than just answer questions. You describe what you want in plain English or assemble it in a visual builder, and the agent can triage your inbox and draft replies, take meeting notes and send follow-ups, update a CRM after a call, qualify inbound leads, answer support tickets or run scheduled routines. Agents connect to 1,500+ apps such as Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Notion and Zendesk, can use a computer for tasks without an API, and can work together.</p>
<p>Lindy was founded by Flo Crivello and has raised about $50M, including a $35M Series B led by Battery Ventures. Pricing is credit-based, with a free trial, Team plans from $29.99/month and an Enterprise plan with HIPAA BAA, SSO and audit logs.</p>
</div>

        <li>Non-technical users who want agents, not workflows</li>

    <div class="verdict-section not-for">

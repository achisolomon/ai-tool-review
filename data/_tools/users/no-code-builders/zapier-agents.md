## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Zapier Agents brings AI-powered autonomous automation to the world's largest integration platform. Unlike basic Zaps that follow fixed triggers and actions, Agents can reason, make decisions, and execute multi-step workflows across 7,000+ connected apps. Users describe what they want in natural language, and the Agent figures out how to accomplish it—pulling data from Salesforce, sending Slack messages, updating spreadsheets, and more. Built on Zapier's decade of workflow expertise, Agents transform complex automation from hours of setup into conversational instructions.</p>
</div>

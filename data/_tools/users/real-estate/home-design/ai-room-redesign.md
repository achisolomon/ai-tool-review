## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>AI Room Redesign is a web studio that takes a single photo of a room and generates redesigned versions of it. You pick the room type (bedroom, living room, bathroom, kitchen, dining room, office), a design style, elements and lighting, and can choose to keep your existing furniture. The same studio also handles house exteriors and gardens. Results can be compared side by side and downloaded. It is aimed at homeowners, renters and hobby decorators who want quick visual concepts before buying furniture or hiring a designer, and the site notes that outputs are visual concepts, not construction drawings.</p>
</div>

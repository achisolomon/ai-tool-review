## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Uizard is a drag-and-drop UI design tool built for people who aren't professional designers. Its AI features generate multi-screen app and web mockups from a text prompt (Autodesigner), convert hand-drawn sketches into digital wireframes, and turn screenshots of existing apps into editable designs. Mockups can be linked into clickable prototypes and shared for feedback, and a large template library speeds up common layouts.</p>
<p>Founded in Copenhagen in 2018, Uizard was acquired by Miro in June 2024, when it had more than 3 million users. It remains available as "Uizard by Miro Labs" with Free, Pro and Business plans, while its technology also feeds Miro's own AI prototyping features.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Stitch is Google's AI tool for designing app and web interfaces. Describe a screen in words, upload a sketch or a screenshot, or talk to it, and Stitch generates polished UI designs, including multi-screen flows on an infinite canvas. You can refine designs conversationally, turn them into clickable prototypes, and export them to Figma or as front-end code (HTML and CSS) to continue in your usual tools.</p>
<p>Stitch launched in 2025 after Google brought in the team behind Galileo AI, an earlier AI UI design startup, and was upgraded in March 2026 with "vibe design" features and newer Gemini models. It is a Google Labs experiment: free with daily credit limits (about 400 design credits and 15 redesign credits per day), with no paid plan yet.</p>
</div>

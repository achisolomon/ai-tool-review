## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Fellow is an AI meeting assistant that addresses the complete meeting workflow—from pre-meeting preparation through live transcription to post-meeting follow-ups. Founded in 2017 by former SurveyMonkey executives, the platform differentiates itself from transcription-only tools by offering collaborative agendas, 500+ meeting templates, and deep integrations with CRMs and project management tools. Named the top pick by The New York Times Wirecutter for transcribing and summarizing meetings, Fellow supports both bot-based and bot-free recording across Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack Huddles. The platform is SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliant, with a commitment that user data is never used to train AI models.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Read.ai is an AI-powered meeting assistant that transforms meetings, emails, and messages into summaries, insights, and instant answers. Founded in 2021 by former leaders from Foursquare, Snapchat, and Placed, the Seattle-based company provides unified AI intelligence across workplace communications. Unlike competitors that focus solely on meeting transcription, Read.ai's "Search Copilot" provides cross-channel search across meetings, emails, Slack, Teams, and documents with citations. The platform includes real-time engagement metrics, sentiment tracking, coaching insights, and Ada—a digital twin AI assistant that handles email writing, scheduling, and workflow automation. Read.ai integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and 20+ enterprise tools including Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, and Asana.</p>
</div>

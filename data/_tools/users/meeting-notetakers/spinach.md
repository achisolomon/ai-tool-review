## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Spinach AI is a meeting automation platform that puts "Your Meetings on Autopilot" by capturing conversations and converting them into actionable intelligence. Founded in 2021 and backed by Y Combinator, Zoom Ventures, and Atlassian, the Nashville-based company provides accurate transcripts and meeting summaries in 100+ languages. Beyond basic transcription, Spinach automatically generates action items, creates tickets in project management tools, updates CRMs, and sends recap emails—positioning itself as a "system of record for conversation data." The platform is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliant, with data encrypted and never used for model training.</p>
</div>

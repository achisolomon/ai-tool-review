## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Fireflies.ai is an AI-powered meeting assistant that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes conversations across video conferencing platforms including Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Founded in 2016, the platform offers 95% accurate transcription in over 100 languages with speaker identification, real-time meeting assistance, and deep integrations with CRM tools like Salesforce and HubSpot. Fireflies is particularly strong for sales teams needing automated meeting logs synced directly to deal records, and is trusted by over 800,000 companies including 75% of Fortune 500 organizations. The platform is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and offers HIPAA compliance on Enterprise plans.</p>
</div>

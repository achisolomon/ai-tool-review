## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>tl;dv (Too Long; Didn't View) is an AI-powered meeting intelligence platform founded in Germany that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Beyond basic transcription, tl;dv enables users to create shareable video clips of key moments, automatically sync meeting insights to CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot, and leverage AI-powered sales coaching with playbook adherence tracking. The platform supports 30+ languages with real-time transcription and offers custom AI summary formats including MEDDIC methodology support. Trusted by enterprises including Salesforce, Cloudflare, Forbes, Deel, and Roche, tl;dv is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant with EU-hosted data centers.</p>
</div>

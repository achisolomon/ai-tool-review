## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Granola is an AI-powered notepad designed for professionals attending back-to-back meetings. Unlike traditional meeting transcription tools that deploy visible bots into calls, Granola transcribes audio directly from your computer's system audio—making it completely invisible to other participants. The platform enhances raw notes into organized, searchable documents with AI-generated summaries, action items, and follow-ups. Founded in 2023 by former Google product managers Chris Pedregal and Sam Stephenson (creators of Socratic, acquired by Google), Granola has rapidly grown to a $1.5B unicorn valuation. It works across Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and Slack, with integrations to Notion, HubSpot, Slack, and CRM systems.</p>
</div>

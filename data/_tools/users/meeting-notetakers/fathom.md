## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Fathom is an AI notetaking platform founded in 2020 by Richard White (Y Combinator W21) that automatically captures and summarizes meetings, eliminating manual note-taking during calls. Used by over 300,000 companies, Fathom works across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, delivering instant AI summaries within 30 seconds of call completion. The platform offers both bot-based and bot-free capture options via its desktop app, with native integrations to CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot, plus ChatGPT and Claude for meeting data analysis. Fathom is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA compliant.</p>
</div>

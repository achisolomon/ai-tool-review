## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>CallingYouNow is an AI-powered call management platform for businesses that handles incoming phone calls around the clock. It answers calls automatically and schedules appointments without a human on the line, operating in 30 or more languages. The AI is trained on a business's services, tone, and FAQs, and the platform analyzes calls afterward for customer insights. The company claims a 5-second response time to incoming calls. CallingYouNow is operated by parent company Fluten Inc. and is based in Glendora, California.</p>
</div>

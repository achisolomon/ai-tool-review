## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Retell AI is a 3rd-generation voice AI platform that enables businesses to build and deploy human-like conversational agents for phone call automation. The platform offers industry-leading ~600ms latency with advanced features including real-time function calling, drag-and-drop call flow design, streaming RAG integration, and AI-powered quality assurance. Retell powers autonomous voice agents for appointment booking, lead qualification, customer support, debt collection, and more. Notable customers include Pine Park Health (38% increase in scheduling NPS), SWTCH (50%+ reduction in support costs), and Medical Data Systems (~$280K monthly in AI-processed collections).</p>
</div>

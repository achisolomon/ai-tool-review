## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>PolyAI builds "the world's most lifelike voice AI agents" for enterprise contact centers. Founded in 2017 in London by a team from Cambridge University, the company has developed a proprietary model called Raven trained on over 1 billion enterprise conversations. PolyAI specializes in complex customer interactions including fraud detection, outage management, and multilingual disputes. The platform offers Agent Studio (no-code builder) and ADK (Agent Development Kit) for developers, with built-in compliance for SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, and PCI DSS. Customer results include $200K+ revenue from a single voice agent (hotel/casino), $1M+ savings from eliminated seasonal hiring (retail), and 100% call resolution without human agents (delivery company).</p>
</div>

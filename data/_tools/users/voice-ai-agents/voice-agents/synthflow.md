## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Synthflow is an enterprise-focused voice AI platform that claims to be "the only end-to-end Voice AI platform with in-house telephony." The platform has processed 65M+ customer calls and saved clients 4M+ hours through automated phone conversations. Synthflow specializes in sub-100ms latency voice agents for appointment scheduling, lead qualification, customer support, and outbound campaigns. Backed by Accel, the platform offers enterprise-grade security (HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR compliance), 50+ language support with regional accents, and integrations with 200+ platforms including HubSpot, Salesforce, and GoHighLevel. Notable results include 35% increase in answered calls for customers.</p>
</div>

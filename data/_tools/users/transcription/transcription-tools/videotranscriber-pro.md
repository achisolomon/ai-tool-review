## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Video Transcriber is an AI speech-to-text platform that converts video and audio into searchable, editable transcripts. It accepts file uploads, public links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Apple Podcasts, and Bilibili, and direct browser recording. It supports 200+ languages with auto-detection and speaker identification and labeling, and layers AI-generated summaries, key points, chapters, and mind maps on top of the raw transcript. Transcripts export as TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Wispr Flow is an AI-powered voice dictation tool from Wispr AI that works in almost any text box across Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. Instead of typing, you hold a hotkey and speak — Flow transcribes your speech, strips filler words like "um" and "uh," adds punctuation, and formats the output to match the app you're writing in. It targets professionals, writers, and anyone who wants to draft faster than they can type, with users reporting speeds of 150–180 words per minute.</p>
</div>

## Key Features

- **Voice typing anywhere**: Dictate into virtually any app or text field with a single hotkey
- **AI cleanup & formatting**: Automatically removes filler words, adds punctuation, and adapts tone to the active app
- **Command Mode**: On Pro plans (Mac/Windows), voice-edit highlighted text — "make this more concise," "translate to Polish," "turn this outline into a paragraph"
- **Custom dictionary**: Add names, jargon, and niche terms so they transcribe correctly instead of being auto-corrected
- **100+ languages**: Supports multilingual dictation, including mixed-language input like Hinglish
- **Cross-device sync**: Settings and dictionary sync across devices on paid plans

## Pros & Cons

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Category-leading AI formatting and filler-word cleanup</li>
      <li>Fast, accurate dictation up to 150–180 WPM in clean conditions</li>
      <li>Works system-wide across Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android</li>
      <li>100+ languages including mixed-language input</li>
      <li>Command Mode enables true voice editing, not just dictation</li>
      <li>Custom dictionary handles names and niche terminology well</li>
      <li>Generous free tier to test the workflow</li>
    </ul>

  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Captures periodic screenshots of the active window for context, sent to the cloud</li>
      <li>No fully offline mode — requires an internet connection</li>
      <li>Windows (Electron) app is RAM/CPU heavy and can freeze target apps</li>
      <li>Subscription-only; no one-time purchase option</li>
      <li>Some users report reliability drops after the free trial</li>
      <li>iOS app can interfere with native dictation and external keyboards</li>
    </ul>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Typeless is an AI voice dictation app that converts speech into ready-to-send text in real time. Unlike raw transcription, it auto-edits as you talk—stripping filler words ("um," "uh"), removing repetition, applying self-corrections, and adapting tone to the app you're writing in. Built by Stanford alumni, it runs natively across macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android—one of the few dictation tools with first-class Android support—and works inside 30+ apps like Slack, Notion, and email.</p>
</div>

## Pros & Cons

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Auto-editing produces clean, send-ready text—not raw transcripts</li>
      <li>Widest platform coverage in AI dictation, including native Android</li>
      <li>Generous 8,000-words/week free tier (about 4x some rivals)</li>
      <li>Strong multilingual support with 100+ languages</li>
      <li>Fast dictation throughput, roughly 4x typing speed</li>
      <li>Tone adapts automatically to the app you're writing in</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://www.typeless.com/" target="_blank">Official Site</a> · <a href="https://spokenly.app/blog/typeless-review" target="_blank">Spokenly Review</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>$30/mo month-to-month is the highest rate in AI dictation</li>
      <li>Cloud-only processing may not suit privacy-sensitive users</li>
      <li>Auto-editing can alter wording when verbatim is needed</li>
      <li>Requires an internet connection to work</li>
      <li>Newer product with a shorter track record</li>
    </ul>

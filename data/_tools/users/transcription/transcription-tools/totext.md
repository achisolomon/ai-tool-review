## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>ToText converts audio and video files into time-aligned, searchable transcripts using AI speech recognition. It supports MP3, MP4, M4A, MOV, WAV, and WebM, with automatic speaker recognition and voice separation, plus AI-generated summaries, meeting notes, chapters, and action items on top of the raw transcript.</p>
</div>

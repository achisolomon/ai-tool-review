## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Brave Search is a privacy-first search engine that operates its own independent index built from scratch, rather than relying on Google or Bing results like most competitors. Launched in 2021, it delivers AI-powered answers with source citations, community-created ranking customization via "Goggles," and anonymous local results without tracking or profiling users. The platform offers multiple LLM model choices including Claude, Llama, and Gemma for its AI chat feature, with all conversations encrypted and auto-deleted after 24 hours. Brave Search processes over 20 billion queries annually and maintains an index of 40+ billion pages updated with 100+ million pages daily.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>You.com is an AI-powered search engine and assistant founded in 2020 by Richard Socher (former Salesforce Chief Scientist) and Bryan McCann. The platform combines real-time web search with multi-model AI access, delivering cited answers with verifiable sources. Unlike traditional search engines, You.com synthesizes information from multiple web sources and presents comprehensive answers with numbered citations. The platform emphasizes privacy, offering an ad-free experience without user tracking. In 2025, You.com achieved unicorn status with a $1.5 billion valuation and has pivoted toward enterprise AI APIs while maintaining its consumer search product.</p>
</div>

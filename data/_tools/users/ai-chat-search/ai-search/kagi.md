## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Kagi Search is a premium, ad-free search engine built on the principle that users should be the customers, not the product. Founded in 2018 by Vladimir Prelovac, Kagi offers a fundamentally different search experience: no ads, no tracking, and AI features designed to enhance rather than monetize your search. With tools like FastGPT for instant AI answers and Universal Summarizer for digesting long content, Kagi combines traditional search quality with modern AI capabilities. The subscription model aligns Kagi's incentives directly with user satisfaction rather than advertiser interests.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Andi is an AI-powered search engine built for the next generation that delivers direct, conversational answers instead of traditional link lists. Unlike Google or Bing, Andi uses its proprietary Trantora AI index that analyzes full page content for meaning and credibility, filtering spam at ingestion rather than after. The platform combines generative AI with multiple language models to synthesize accurate, trustworthy answers from high-quality sources. With a privacy-first design offering no ads, no tracking, and no data collection, Andi has been recognized by PCMag as Best Free AI Search Engine for three consecutive years (2024-2026).</p>
</div>

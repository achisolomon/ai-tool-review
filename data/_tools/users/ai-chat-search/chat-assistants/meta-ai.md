## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Meta AI is a free conversational AI assistant built by Meta Platforms, available across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and as a standalone web app at meta.ai. Powered by the Muse Spark model (successor to Llama 4), it enables users to get instant answers, generate images with the Imagine feature, create AI videos with Movie Gen, and tackle complex tasks with advanced reasoning modes. With 1.2 billion monthly active users as of Q1 2026, Meta AI has become the second-largest AI assistant globally after ChatGPT, with nearly two-thirds of engagements occurring on WhatsApp.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Google Gemini (formerly Bard) is Google's flagship AI assistant powered by the Gemini family of multimodal models. What sets it apart is seamless integration with Google Workspace—Gemini can draft emails in Gmail, summarize documents in Drive, create presentations in Slides, and analyze spreadsheets in Sheets. With Gemini 2.0, it offers native multimodal understanding (text, images, code, audio), real-time web access through Google Search, and the ability to generate images. For Google ecosystem users, it's the most natural choice for an AI assistant that works across your existing tools.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Qwen Chat (Qwen Studio) is Alibaba Cloud's free AI assistant, powered by the Qwen family of large language models. Available on web, iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows, it offers multilingual support across 119 languages, multimodal capabilities (text, images, audio, video), and hybrid thinking modes that balance speed with depth of reasoning. The platform includes web search, code generation, image editing, and agent capabilities. Backed by open-source models with over 700 million downloads on Hugging Face, Qwen has become the world's most downloaded open-source AI model family, particularly strong for coding, multilingual tasks, and Chinese/Japanese/Korean text processing.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>DeepSeek Chat is a free AI assistant from Chinese AI startup DeepSeek that shocked the industry in January 2025 by releasing reasoning models matching OpenAI's o1 at 95% lower cost. The web interface offers unlimited free access to both DeepSeek R1 (reasoning model with chain-of-thought) and DeepSeek V3 (general assistant). R1's transparent thinking process, where you can watch the model reason step-by-step, has made it popular for math, coding, and complex problem-solving. The open-source model weights and rock-bottom API pricing have positioned DeepSeek as the cost-effective alternative to Western AI providers.</p>
</div>

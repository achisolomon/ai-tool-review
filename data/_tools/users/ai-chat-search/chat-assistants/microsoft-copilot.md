## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant powered by GPT-4 and integrated across Windows 11, Microsoft Edge, and the entire Microsoft 365 suite. Originally launched as Bing Chat in 2023, it evolved into Copilot with deep OS-level integration, allowing users to ask questions, generate images with DALL-E 3, search the web with Bing, and automate tasks directly from their desktop or browser. For enterprise users, Microsoft 365 Copilot transforms productivity apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Teams with AI-powered drafting, data analysis, and meeting summaries.</p>
</div>

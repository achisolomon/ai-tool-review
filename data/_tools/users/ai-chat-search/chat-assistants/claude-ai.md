## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Claude is Anthropic's flagship AI assistant, built with a focus on safety, helpfulness, and honesty. What sets Claude apart is its industry-leading 1 million token context window, allowing it to process entire codebases, lengthy documents, and extended conversations without losing track. Claude excels at nuanced writing, thoughtful analysis, complex coding tasks, and multi-turn conversations that require maintaining context. With Projects for organizing work, Artifacts for code and documents, and MCP integrations for connecting to external tools, Claude offers a refined experience for professionals who need depth over breadth.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Claude Design is an Anthropic Labs product launched on April 17, 2026 that lets you create polished visual work — prototypes, pitch decks, slides, one-pagers, and landing pages — through conversation with Claude. Unlike AI image generators that produce static pixels, Claude Design outputs live HTML, CSS, and React components that are interactive from the start. It is powered by Claude Opus 4.7 and is available in research preview for Claude Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise subscribers. Presentations have become one of the most popular internal use cases at Anthropic, enabling teams to build professional decks in minutes rather than hours.</p>
</div>

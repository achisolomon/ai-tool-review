## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Gemini Canvas is Google's dedicated workspace inside the Gemini app that generates complete slide presentations from a single prompt. Rolled out broadly on April 11, 2026, it uses Nano Banana (Google's image generation model) for custom visuals. Upload a document or describe a topic, and Gemini builds a full deck with themes, AI-generated images, and visual structure. Then export to Google Slides, PowerPoint, or PDF with one click. Canvas works side-by-side with your chat, so you can iterate without switching applications.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use Gemini Canvas?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Google Workspace users needing quick decks</li>
        <li>Business presentations from documents</li>
        <li>Teams wanting easy export to Slides/PPT</li>
        <li>First drafts that need human polish</li>
        <li>Users already paying for Google AI Pro</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Final, polished decks (needs 30-50% editing time)</li>
        <li>Brand-specific designs (images are generic)</li>
        <li>Mobile-first workflows (desktop only for editing)</li>
        <li>Users outside Global/US/EU regions</li>
      </ul>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>One-click export to Google Slides, PowerPoint, PDF</li>
      <li>AI-generated images via Nano Banana</li>
      <li>Side-by-side editing with chat</li>
      <li>Can reference uploaded documents and files</li>
      <li>"Change length" and "Change tone" sliders</li>
      <li>Available to all Gemini users (free tier included)</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-canvas" target="_blank">Google Cloud Docs</a> · <a href="https://findskill.ai/blog/gemini-canvas-presentations-tutorial/" target="_blank">FindSkill.ai</a></div>

  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Output is a first draft—budget 30-50% for editing</li>
      <li>Generated images are hard to modify (Nano Banana limitation)</li>
      <li>Images rarely match brand guidelines</li>
      <li>PowerPoint export sometimes has formatting issues</li>
      <li>Each update creates new artifact (no versioning)</li>
      <li>Mobile: view only, no editing</li>
    </ul>

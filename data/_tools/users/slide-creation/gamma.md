## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Gamma is an AI-native tool for creating presentations, documents, and websites. Type a prompt or paste an outline and Gamma generates a complete, polished deck in seconds — handling layout, design, and structure automatically. Founded in 2020 and launched publicly in 2022, Gamma reached $100M ARR profitably with 70 million users and a $2.1B valuation after its Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz in November 2025. Unlike traditional slide software, Gamma outputs web-based presentations that can be shared as links and embed video, apps, and live websites directly.</p>
</div>

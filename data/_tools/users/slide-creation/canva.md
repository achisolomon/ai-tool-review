## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Canva is an online visual design platform founded in 2012 by Melanie Perkins, Cliff Obrecht, and Cameron Adams in Sydney, Australia. At its April 2026 Canva Create event, the company relaunched itself as "an AI platform with design tools" — a shift from its previous identity as a design platform with AI features. Its Magic Studio AI suite, built into every plan, includes Dream Lab (text-to-image), Magic Write (AI copywriting), Magic Eraser, Magic Expand, and Background Remover. Canva ended 2025 with 265 million monthly active users, $3.5 billion in revenue, and a $42 billion private valuation — ranking as the world's 3rd most-used generative AI web product by monthly active users.</p>
</div>

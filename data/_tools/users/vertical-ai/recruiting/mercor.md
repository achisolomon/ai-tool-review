AI-native recruiting marketplace for tech talent

## Overview
Mercor is an AI-first recruiting marketplace focused on connecting remote tech professionals with companies. Rather than traditional resumes, Mercor uses AI-powered assessments and skill-based matching to evaluate candidates and make recommendations. The platform emphasizes transparency and fair evaluation.

## Key Features
- AI-powered skill assessment and matching
- Remote-first candidate evaluation
- Automated technical screening
- Transparent salary recommendations
- Marketplace with direct company listings
- Candidate skill profiles and growth tracking

## Pricing
Commission-based model: companies pay a percentage of the first-year salary when hiring through the platform. Typical rates range from 15-25% commission.

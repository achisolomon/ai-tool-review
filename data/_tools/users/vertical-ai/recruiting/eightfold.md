Talent intelligence and workforce optimization

## Overview
Eightfold is an enterprise AI platform focused on talent intelligence. It helps organizations identify, match, and develop talent across their entire workforce. The platform uses AI to analyze skills, predict career paths, and recommend internal mobility opportunities in addition to external hiring.

## Key Features
- Candidate-to-role matching using deep skill analysis
- Internal mobility and succession planning
- Skill-based talent marketplace
- Career pathing recommendations
- ATS integration and optimization
- Diversity and inclusion analytics

## Pricing
Enterprise licensing model with pricing based on organization size and number of employees. Custom quotes for implementations starting at $100,000+ annually.

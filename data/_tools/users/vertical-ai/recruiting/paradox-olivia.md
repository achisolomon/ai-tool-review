Conversational AI recruiting assistant

## Overview
Paradox is an HR tech company best known for Olivia, a conversational AI recruiter. Olivia engages with candidates through chat and messaging interfaces, answering questions, conducting initial screening interviews, and moving qualified candidates through the hiring pipeline. The platform works 24/7 to engage candidates and reduce time-to-hire.

## Key Features
- 24/7 conversational candidate engagement
- Automated initial screening and qualifying
- Applicant tracking system (ATS) integration
- Multi-channel communication (chat, SMS, email)
- Scheduling and interview coordination
- Candidate feedback and experience analytics

## Pricing
Subscription model based on monthly active candidates and number of job requisitions. Mid-market implementations typically range from $2,000-5,000 monthly.

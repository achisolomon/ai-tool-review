## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Figma AI brings AI throughout the design-to-code pipeline — directly on the Figma canvas. It includes <strong>Figma Make</strong> (prompt-to-prototype), image generation powered by Gemini 3.0 Pro and GPT Image 1, a canvas-based AI agent for bulk edits and design system application, FigJam AI for diagramming and feedback sorting, and an MCP server that connects design context to AI coding tools like Cursor, VS Code, and Claude.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use Figma AI?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Product designers who already live in Figma and want AI without switching tools</li>
        <li>Design-engineering teams who want design context flowing into their AI coding workflow via MCP</li>
        <li>Product teams who need to rapidly prototype and pressure-test ideas before committing to dev</li>
        <li>Enterprises who need AI governance and design system enforcement at scale</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Teams not already in the Figma ecosystem — AI features require an active Figma subscription</li>
        <li>Developers who want a standalone AI coding assistant (look at Cursor or Claude Code instead)</li>
        <li>Heavy image generation workflows — credit limits apply at every tier</li>
        <li>Budget-conscious teams: AI credits are consumed fast on higher-tier features</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>AI is embedded in the canvas — no context switching to a separate tool</li>
      <li>Figma Make generates interactive prototypes from natural language prompts</li>
      <li>MCP server brings live design context into AI coding tools (VS Code, Cursor, Claude)</li>
      <li>Image generation uses frontier models (Gemini 3.0 Pro, GPT Image 1)</li>
      <li>Canvas AI agent can apply design systems and run bulk edits automatically</li>
      <li>FigJam AI handles diagramming, feedback sorting, and team brainstorming</li>
      <li>Free tier includes 500 AI credits/month — enough to evaluate the toolset</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://www.figma.com/ai/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Figma AI Official</a> · <a href="https://www.figma.com/blog/figma-make/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Figma Make Blog</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>AI features are credit-gated — heavy use on lower plans hits limits fast</li>
      <li>Figma Make outputs are prototypes, not production-ready code</li>
      <li>All AI tiers require an existing Figma subscription — no standalone AI product</li>
      <li>Organization and Enterprise plans required for advanced design system governance</li>
      <li>MCP and advanced API integrations limited to Professional tier and above</li>
    </ul>

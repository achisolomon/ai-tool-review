Voice-based clinical assistant for healthcare providers

## Overview
Suki is an AI-powered clinical assistant designed specifically for healthcare professionals. It uses voice recognition and natural language processing to transcribe patient conversations in real-time and automate electronic health record (EHR) documentation. The platform integrates with major EHR systems and helps reduce administrative burden on clinicians.

## Key Features
- Real-time voice transcription of clinical conversations
- Automatic EHR documentation generation
- EHR system integrations
- HIPAA-compliant operations
- Multi-language support

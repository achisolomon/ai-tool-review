AI data enrichment and sales automation platform

## Overview
Clay is an AI-native platform designed for sales and marketing teams that combines real-time data enrichment with automated outbound workflows. It integrates data from hundreds of sources using AI to build comprehensive prospect profiles and execute highly personalized outreach campaigns.

## Key Features
- Real-time AI-powered data enrichment from 100+ sources
- Automated workflow builder for outbound campaigns
- Personalization at scale with dynamic variable insertion
- Integrations with email, LinkedIn, and CRM platforms
- Built-in AI for lead research and copywriting
- Campaign analytics and performance tracking

## Pricing
Subscription model starting at approximately $99/month for individuals. Team plans and enterprise pricing available based on data enrichment volume and outbound volume.

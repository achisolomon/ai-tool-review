AI digital sales development representatives

## Overview
11x provides AI-powered digital SDRs named Alice and Mike that automate outbound prospecting on behalf of sales teams. These AI agents conduct research, write personalized outreach messages, manage responses, and book meetings with qualified leads. The platform handles the full SDR workflow.

## Key Features
- AI-powered prospecting and research
- Personalized outreach email generation
- Multi-channel engagement (email, LinkedIn)
- Response management and follow-up automation
- Meeting booking and calendar integration
- Real-time reporting and analytics

## Pricing
Performance-based pricing: companies pay when the AI books qualified meetings, typically $100-500 per qualified meeting booked depending on industry and deal size.

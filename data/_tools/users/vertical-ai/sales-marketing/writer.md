Enterprise AI platform for marketing and communications

## Overview
Writer is an enterprise-grade generative AI platform designed specifically for marketing and communications teams. It emphasizes data privacy, brand consistency, and security while providing AI-powered content generation for emails, social media, ads, and other marketing materials.

## Key Features
- Brand-controlled AI content generation
- On-premise and private deployment options
- Data privacy with no training data retention
- Fine-tuned AI models for brand voice
- Integration with marketing tools and workflows
- Team collaboration and approval workflows
- Usage analytics and compliance tracking

## Pricing
Enterprise licensing model with custom pricing based on deployment type (cloud or on-premise) and organizational size. Typical implementations start at $50,000+ annually.

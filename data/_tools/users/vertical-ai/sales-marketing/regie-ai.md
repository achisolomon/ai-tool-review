AI sales automation and content generation platform

## Overview
Regie.ai is an AI-powered sales automation platform that generates personalized outreach messages and automates sales workflows. It uses AI to create customized emails, LinkedIn messages, and sales cadences tailored to each prospect while managing follow-ups and engagement tracking.

## Key Features
- AI-generated personalized sales messages
- Multi-channel campaign automation (email, LinkedIn)
- Dynamic variable insertion for personalization
- Cadence and follow-up automation
- Integration with CRM and email platforms
- Sales team collaboration and approval workflows
- Performance analytics and A/B testing

## Pricing
Subscription-based starting at approximately $99/month for individual users. Team and enterprise plans available with pricing based on number of users and outreach volume.

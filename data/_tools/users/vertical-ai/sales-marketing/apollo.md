AI-powered sales engagement platform

## Overview
Apollo is a sales engagement and intelligence platform that combines a prospecting database with AI-driven outreach automation. It helps sales teams identify ideal customer profiles, find buyers, and automate multi-channel outreach sequences. The platform integrates with CRMs and provides real-time engagement analytics.

## Key Features
- Comprehensive B2B prospecting database (200M+ contacts)
- AI-powered lead scoring and recommendations
- Automated outreach sequencing (email, call, LinkedIn)
- Buyer intent signals and engagement tracking
- CRM integration and data synchronization
- Sales analytics and forecasting

## Pricing
Subscription-based with plans starting at $49/user/month. Enterprise plans based on number of users and data usage, typically $2,000-10,000+ monthly.

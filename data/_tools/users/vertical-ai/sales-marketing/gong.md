## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Gong is the leading revenue intelligence platform that captures every customer interaction—calls, emails, web conferences—and uses AI to analyze conversations, surface insights, and predict deal outcomes. Unlike basic call recording tools, Gong's AI understands sales conversations: it identifies winning behaviors, flags deal risks, tracks competitor mentions, and provides coaching recommendations. The platform has become essential for enterprise sales teams, providing visibility into what actually happens in customer conversations rather than relying on rep-entered CRM data. With features spanning conversation intelligence, deal intelligence, revenue forecasting, and sales engagement, Gong aims to be the single source of truth for go-to-market teams.</p>
</div>

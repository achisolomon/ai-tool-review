AI contract lifecycle management platform

## Overview
Ironclad is an enterprise contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform powered by AI. It helps organizations manage all aspects of the contract lifecycle from creation and negotiation through execution and renewal. The platform uses AI to extract key terms, identify risks, and automate routine tasks.

## Key Features
- AI-powered contract analysis and risk identification
- Automated key term extraction
- Negotiation workflow automation
- Contract repository and search
- Obligation management and tracking
- Integration with major enterprise systems

## Pricing
Enterprise subscription model with pricing based on contract volume and user count. Annual contracts starting at $50,000+ for mid-market organizations.

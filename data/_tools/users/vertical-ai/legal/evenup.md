Personal injury demand letter automation

## Overview
EvenUp is an AI legal assistant specifically designed for personal injury attorneys. It automates the creation of demand letters by analyzing case details and generating comprehensive settlement demands with supporting documentation. The platform helps streamline the settlement negotiation process and ensure consistent, thorough demand packages.

## Key Features
- Automated demand letter generation
- Case valuation assistance
- Medical record analysis
- Settlement demand optimization
- Litigation support tools

## Pricing
Usage-based pricing model. Attorneys pay per demand letter generated or annual subscription plans for law firms handling high volumes of personal injury cases.

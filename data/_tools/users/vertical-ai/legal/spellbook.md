AI-powered contract drafting assistant

## Overview
Spellbook is an AI legal assistant that focuses on contract drafting and review. Built for attorneys, it uses AI to generate contract language, suggest improvements, and identify potential issues. The tool integrates with Microsoft Word and works within existing legal workflows.

## Key Features
- AI-powered contract drafting
- Real-time contract review and suggestions
- Clause library and templates
- Risk identification in contract language
- Microsoft Word integration
- Lawyer feedback training

## Pricing
Monthly subscription starting at $30-40/month for individual attorneys. Enterprise plans available for law firms with volume pricing.

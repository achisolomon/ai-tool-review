## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Intercom is an AI-first customer service platform that combines live chat, help desk, and AI-powered automation in one unified system. Founded in 2011, Intercom pioneered the modern business messenger and has evolved into a comprehensive support platform serving 25,000+ companies including Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Atlassian. Their Fin AI agent, launched in 2023, uses GPT-4 to resolve customer queries instantly with accurate, conversational responses sourced from your help center content. Unlike rule-based chatbots, Fin understands context, handles complex multi-turn conversations, and seamlessly hands off to human agents when needed.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Vedic Astrology Chart generates sidereal (Vedic) birth charts from a user's birth date, time, and location, using the Swiss Ephemeris for planetary position calculations. The output includes planetary positions, house placements, nakshatras, and 16 divisional charts, including the D9 Navamsa chart used for marriage analysis and the D10 Dasamsa chart used for career analysis. It also calculates the Vimshottari Dasha planetary period sequence and flags yogas (planetary combinations) such as Raja Yoga, Dhana Yoga, and Mangal Dosha. Beyond the core chart, the site offers transit analysis, a compatibility checker, a Sade Sati calculator, and a moon sign calculator. An AI layer turns the raw chart data into written interpretation of personality, career, relationship, and spiritual themes. The site itself describes this interpretation as intended for self-reflection, not as a substitute for consultation with an experienced Vedic astrologer.</p>
</div>

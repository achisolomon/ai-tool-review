## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>OpenAI dots is OpenAI's always-on personal agent, announced at DevDay on September 29, 2026. Unlike a chat session, a dot runs continuously on its own cloud computer, pursuing goals a user sets with minimal ongoing supervision. Users can name and customize their primary dot, message it through ChatGPT, Slack, or Microsoft Teams, and connect it to more than 4,000 apps via ChatGPT's plugin ecosystem. OpenAI positions dots as a step from an "answering AI" toward a "working AI," with plans for teams of specialist dots collaborating on a user's behalf over time.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use OpenAI dots?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Pro or Business Premium subscribers who want a standing agent monitoring feedback, inboxes, or workflows around the clock</li>
        <li>Developers delegating recurring tasks like triaging bug reports or re-running analyses as new data arrives</li>
        <li>Teams already working inside Slack or Microsoft Teams who want an agent reachable from those tools</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Free, Go, or Plus ChatGPT users; no dot is included below the Pro tier</li>
        <li>Users in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK, where the Pro-tier rollout is currently excluded</li>
        <li>Anyone wary of a largely autonomous agent with broad app access and minimal real-time oversight</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Runs independently of any single device or interface, working in the background 24/7</li>
      <li>Conversations with a dot don't count against standard ChatGPT usage limits</li>
      <li>Reachable from ChatGPT, Slack, and Microsoft Teams, with text messaging planned</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/" target="_blank">Source</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Gated to Pro ($100+/month) and Business Premium plans; not available on Free, Go, or Plus</li>
      <li>When a dot delegates work to Codex or ChatGPT, that usage draws from the same shared plan allowance, so overnight tasks can exhaust it before a user wakes up</li>
      <li>OpenAI withheld a planned model update ahead of launch over alignment concerns, and has acknowledged tension between shipping autonomous agents quickly and ensuring adequate oversight</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/openai-launches-dots-ai-agents-safety-questions-rcna600338" target="_blank">Source</a></div>

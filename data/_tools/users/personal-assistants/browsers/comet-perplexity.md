## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Comet is Perplexity's web browser. It is built on Chromium, so Chrome extensions and bookmarks carry over, but every page has the Comet Assistant beside it. The assistant can answer questions about what you're reading, summarize long pages and videos, compare information across tabs, and in agent mode carry out multi-step tasks such as filling forms, collecting information from several sites, or drafting emails. Search goes through Perplexity, so answers come with citations.</p>
<p>Comet launched in July 2025 for Perplexity's top-tier subscribers and has since become free for everyone, with paid Perplexity plans unlocking higher limits and stronger models. Comet Plus, a $5/month add-on, gives access to content from partner publishers.</p>
</div>

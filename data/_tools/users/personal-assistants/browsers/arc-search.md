## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Arc Search is the mobile companion to the Arc desktop browser, made by The Browser Company. Its signature feature, Browse for Me, turns a search into a small research task: an AI agent opens several relevant pages, reads them, and assembles a single clean answer page with headings, bullet points, images and source links. The app also blocks ads and trackers by default and archives tabs automatically.</p>
<p>Know the context before adopting it. The Browser Company put Arc into maintenance mode in May 2025 to focus on its newer AI browser, Dia, and was acquired by Atlassian for $610M in 2025. Browse for Me still ships in Arc Search, but new AI work happens in Dia, so expect fixes rather than new features.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Instinct is a personal AI assistant built by Spear Street Technology, Inc., founded by Noah Shinn, a former research scientist at Sierra. Rather than a dedicated app, Instinct works entirely through text messages and phone calls: users text or call it to dispute a bill, order groceries, book a handyman, schedule appointments, or plan a trip. It runs on a persistent cloud computer with access to connected email, messaging, calendar, and other accounts, letting it follow up on tasks and reach back out proactively over hours without further prompting. The product is currently invite-only and in private beta.</p>
</div>

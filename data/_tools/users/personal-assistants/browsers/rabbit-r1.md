## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>The Rabbit R1 is a small orange handheld AI device with a 2.88-inch touchscreen, a rotating camera, a scroll wheel and a push-to-talk button. You talk to it, point the camera at things, and it answers or acts. It was unveiled at CES in January 2024 at $199 with no subscription and sold well, but launch reviews were harsh: early software was unreliable and the promised ability to operate apps for you largely didn't work.</p>
<p>Rabbit, led by founder and CEO Jesse Lyu, kept shipping updates and in September 2025 released rabbitOS 2, a full redesign around colorful cards. It added on-screen conversations that mix voice, text and images, an agent called Intern that runs on the device, and "creations" for building small tools and games by talking to it. Reviewers called it a significant improvement, though the R1 remains a companion gadget rather than a phone replacement.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Dia is an AI browser from The Browser Company, the team behind Arc, and the product the company now puts its effort into after moving Arc to maintenance mode. Dia looks like a simple Chrome-style browser, but an assistant sits in the sidebar and can read your open tabs: you can ask it to compare products across tabs, summarize a long document, draft a reply from what you're reading, or explain something on the page. Skills let you save reusable prompts as shortcuts, and memory lets Dia learn your preferences over time.</p>
<p>Dia entered beta in June 2025 and added a $20/month Pro plan in August 2025 that removes AI usage limits. The Browser Company was acquired by Atlassian in 2025, and Dia is increasingly positioned for knowledge work. It runs on macOS; Windows is still on a waitlist.</p>
</div>

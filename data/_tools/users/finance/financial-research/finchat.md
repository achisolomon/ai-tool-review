## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Fiscal.ai is the new name of FinChat, an AI research terminal for stock investors. It started as a chat interface for financial questions and has grown into a data platform that combines standardized fundamentals, segment and KPI data, filings and earnings call transcripts with an AI copilot that answers multi-part questions in plain language and cites its sources. The company also sells its data through an API and MCP server for developers. It raised a $10M Series A led by Portage in 2025, bringing total funding to about $13M, and reports more than 350,000 registered users.</p>
<p>The pitch is institutional-grade fundamental data at a retail price, as a lighter alternative to a Bloomberg or FactSet seat for individual investors and small funds.</p>
</div>

## The Verdict

<div class="verdict">
  <h3>Who Should Use Fiscal.ai?</h3>
  <div class="verdict-grid">
    <div class="verdict-section">
      <h4>Best For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Individual investors doing fundamental research</li>
        <li>Analysts who live in earnings calls and KPI tables</li>
        <li>Small funds that can't justify a Bloomberg terminal</li>
        <li>Developers who need financial data through an API or MCP</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="verdict-section not-for">
      <h4>Not Ideal For</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Day traders who need real-time quotes or Level 2 data</li>
        <li>Technical analysis and advanced charting</li>
        <li>Investment banking deal work (see Rogo or Hebbia)</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="pros-cons">
  <div class="pros-list">
    <h3>What's Great</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Finance-specific AI that handles detailed fundamental questions better than general chatbots</li>
      <li>Coverage of 100,000+ global public companies, with deep segment and KPI breakdowns for about 2,000</li>
      <li>Summarizes earnings calls and filings in seconds, with citations</li>
      <li>Generous free tier, with a trial of the paid plan for new users</li>
      <li>Far cheaper than traditional institutional terminals</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="source"><a href="https://www.wallstreetzen.com/blog/finchat-io-fiscal-ai-review/" target="_blank">WallStreetZen review</a> - <a href="https://www.fintipster.com/fiscal-ai-review/" target="_blank">FinTipster review</a></div>
  </div>
  <div class="cons-list">
    <h3>Watch Out For</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>No real-time prices, no order-book data, limited charting</li>
      <li>Prices have risen several times since the FinChat days</li>
      <li>Deep KPI coverage is limited to a subset of companies</li>
      <li>Built for public equities; little for private markets</li>
    </ul>

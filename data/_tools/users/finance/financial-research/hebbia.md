## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Hebbia is an AI platform for document-heavy finance work. Its core product, Matrix, lays out a research question as a grid: each row is a document (a CIM, a 10-K, a credit agreement, a data-room file) and each column is a question, and AI agents fill every cell with an answer that links back to the exact passage it came from. That makes it practical to compare hundreds or thousands of documents side by side instead of reading them one at a time.</p>
<p>Founded in 2020 by George Sivulka, Hebbia raised a $130M Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz in 2024 at a $700M valuation. TechCrunch reported at the time that about 30% of the top 50 asset managers by AUM were customers. It also connects to licensed data such as FactSet, S&amp;P Capital IQ and PitchBook, alongside a firm's own files.</p>
</div>

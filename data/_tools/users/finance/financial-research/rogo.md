## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>Rogo builds AI for investment banking, private equity and public-markets investing. It works like a junior analyst that understands finance: it pulls company and market data, drafts research and diligence memos, and produces output inside Excel, PowerPoint and Word, where deal teams already work. Answers are grounded in licensed data (FactSet, PitchBook and others) and in a firm's own documents and CRM, with an audit trail.</p>
<p>Rogo was founded in New York in 2021 by Gabriel Stengel and John Willett. It has raised over $300M from investors including Thrive Capital, Sequoia and Kleiner Perkins, reached a reported $2B valuation in 2026, and reports more than 350 institutions and 50,000 users, including banks such as Rothschild, Lazard, Jefferies, Moelis and Nomura.</p>
</div>

## Overview

<div class="overview">
<p>RingCentral is a cloud-based unified communications platform that brands itself as "The Agentic Voice AI platform to drive customer outcomes." Founded by Vlad Shmunis and Vlad Vendrow, the company pioneered cloud-based business communications and now integrates AI throughout its product suite. RingCentral's offerings include RingEX (unified communications with calling, messaging, and meetings), RingCX (AI-powered contact center), and AI Receptionist technology that automates call handling. With approximately 8,000 employees globally, RingCentral serves over 600,000 businesses across 46 countries, from small businesses to large enterprises, all powered by AI that can "automate, assist, and analyze customer interactions end-to-end."</p>
</div>
